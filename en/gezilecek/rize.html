Things to see in Rize

Museums, archaeological sites, historic buildings and natural sights

Home › Things to see, province by province › Rize

The Kültür Varlıkları guide lists 5 places in Rize: 2 museum, 1 rafting, 1 hiking trail, 1 castle & historic building. Below you will find the highlights, a map and the full list.

⭐ Highlights

1 · Rize · MuseumRize Atatürk House Museum

Rize Atatürk House Museum is a historic villa on the hillside where Atatürk spent a night during his 1924 visit.

2 · Rize · MuseumRize Museum

Rize Museum presents the heritage of Rize, known for green valleys and tea gardens.

3 · Rize · RaftingFırtına Deresi

The Fırtına stream in Rize's Çamlıhemşin district comes down from the Kaçkar Mountains and is known for its green valley and stone arch bridges.
