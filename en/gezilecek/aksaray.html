Things to see in Aksaray

Museums, archaeological sites, historic buildings and natural sights

Home › Things to see, province by province › Aksaray

The Kültür Varlıkları guide lists 12 places in Aksaray: 4 archaeological site, 3 castle & historic building, 2 underground city, 1 museum, 1 religious building, 1 lake. Below you will find the highlights, a map and the full list.

⭐ Highlights

1 · Aksaray · Archaeological siteAcemhöyük

Acemhöyük, at Yeşilova northwest of Aksaray, is a vast mound that was one of Anatolia's greatest trading centres in the Bronze Age.

2 · Aksaray · Archaeological siteMonastery Valley, Aksaray

Monastery Valley stretches beside Güzelyurt and is filled with churches, chapels and dwellings carved into the rock.

3 · Aksaray · MuseumAksaray Museum

Aksaray Museum brings together thousands of years of history of Aksaray, the western gateway to Cappadocia.
