Things to see in Muğla

Museums, archaeological sites, historic buildings and natural sights

Home › Things to see, province by province › Muğla

The Kültür Varlıkları guide lists 48 places in Muğla: 32 archaeological site, 9 museum, 2 lake, 2 hiking trail, 1 rafting, 1 castle & historic building, 1 canyon. Below you will find the highlights, a map and the full list.

⭐ Highlights

1 · Muğla · Archaeological siteAmintas Kaya Mezarı

The Rock Tomb of Amyntas, carved into the cliffs above central Fethiye, is one of Lycia's most impressive rock tombs.

2 · Muğla · Archaeological siteAncient City of Amos

Amos, near Turunç in Marmaris, is a Hellenistic Carian town on a hill overlooking Kumlubük bay.

3 · Muğla · Archaeological siteAncient City of Arsada

Arsada is a Lycian city on a hill above the Eşen valley near Arsaköy in Seydikemer, between Tlos and Xanthos.
