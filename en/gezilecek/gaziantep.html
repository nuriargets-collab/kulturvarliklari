Things to see in Gaziantep

Museums, archaeological sites, historic buildings and natural sights

Home › Things to see, province by province › Gaziantep

The Kültür Varlıkları guide lists 14 places in Gaziantep: 7 archaeological site, 5 museum, 2 castle & historic building. Below you will find the highlights, a map and the full list.

⭐ Highlights

1 · Gaziantep · MuseumZeugma Mosaic Museum

Zeugma Mosaic Museum is one of the largest mosaic museums in the world, displaying extraordinary works from ancient Zeugma on the Euphrates.

2 · Gaziantep · MuseumGaziantep Archaeology Museum

Gaziantep Archaeological Museum presents thousands of years of history of Gaziantep and its surroundings, a transition zone between Mesopotamia and Anatolia.

3 · Gaziantep · MuseumGaziantep Savunması and Kahramanlık Panorama Museum

The Gaziantep Defence and Heroism Panorama Museum, inside Gaziantep Castle, tells the story of the city's defence in 1920–1921.
