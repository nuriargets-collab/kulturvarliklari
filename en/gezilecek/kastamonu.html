⭐ Highlights

1 · Kastamonu · MuseumKastamonu Museum

Kastamonu Museum presents the past of the region known in antiquity as Paphlagonia.

2 · Kastamonu · CanyonHorma Canyon

Horma Canyon is in Küre Mountains National Park in Kastamonu's Pınarbaşı district.

3 · Kastamonu · Ski resortIlgaz Kayak Merkezi

Ilgaz Ski Resort is in Mount Ilgaz National Park between Kastamonu and Çankırı.
