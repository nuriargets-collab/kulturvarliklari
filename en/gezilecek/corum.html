Things to see in Çorum

Museums, archaeological sites, historic buildings and natural sights

Home › Things to see, province by province › Çorum

The Kültür Varlıkları guide lists 7 places in Çorum: 4 archaeological site, 2 museum, 1 hiking trail. Below you will find the highlights, a map and the full list.

⭐ Highlights

1 · Çorum · Archaeological siteHattusa

Hattusa was the capital of the Hittite Empire, a great Bronze Age power, and for some five hundred years governed a state stretching from Anatolia to Syria.

2 · Çorum · MuseumBoğazköy Museum

Boğazköy Museum, close to the Hattusa site, displays finds from the Hittite capital.

3 · Çorum · Archaeological siteAlacahöyük Museum and Archaeological Site

Alacahöyük, near the Hittite capital Hattusa, is one of Anatolia's most important Bronze Age centres.
