Things to see in Eskişehir

Museums, archaeological sites, historic buildings and natural sights

Home › Things to see, province by province › Eskişehir

The Kültür Varlıkları guide lists 15 places in Eskişehir: 11 museum, 2 archaeological site, 1 hiking trail, 1 religious building. Below you will find the highlights, a map and the full list.

⭐ Highlights

1 · Eskişehir · MuseumAhşap Eserler Museum

The Museum of Woodworking is a museum-gallery in Eskişehir's Odunpazarı, west of the Kurşunlu Complex in a historic quarter full of museums.

2 · Eskişehir · MuseumDevrim Arabaları Museum

The Devrim Cars Museum, on the TÜRASAŞ (formerly TÜLOMSAŞ) site in Eskişehir, tells the story of "Devrim", Turkey's first domestic car, built in just 129 days in 1961.

3 · Eskişehir · MuseumEskişehir Kurtuluş Museum

The Eskişehir Liberation Museum in Odunpazarı, opened in 2016, covers the Eskişehir front of the War of Independence.
