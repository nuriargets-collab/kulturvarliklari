Things to see in Trabzon

Museums, archaeological sites, historic buildings and natural sights

Home › Things to see, province by province › Trabzon

The Kültür Varlıkları guide lists 6 places in Trabzon: 3 religious building, 2 museum, 1 lake. Below you will find the highlights, a map and the full list.

⭐ Highlights

1 · Trabzon · MuseumTrabzon Atatürk Pavilion

The Trabzon Atatürk Pavilion, on a hill in Soğuksu surrounded by pines and lindens, is a white villa built in the 1890s for a Greek banking family.

2 · Trabzon · MuseumTrabzon Museum (Kostaki Mansion)

Trabzon Museum is housed in a grand European-style mansion built in the early 20th century for the Greek banker Kostaki Theophylaktos.

3 · Trabzon · Religious buildingKüçük Ayvasıl St. Anna Church

Little Ayvasıl (St Anne's) Church, in historic Trabzon, is considered one of the city's oldest surviving Byzantine buildings.
