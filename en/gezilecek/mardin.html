Things to see in Mardin

Museums, archaeological sites, historic buildings and natural sights

Home › Things to see, province by province › Mardin

The Kültür Varlıkları guide lists 6 places in Mardin: 3 religious building, 2 museum, 1 archaeological site. Below you will find the highlights, a map and the full list.

⭐ Highlights

1 · Mardin · Archaeological siteDara Archaeological Site

Dara, south-east of Mardin, was a powerful frontier garrison city founded by the Byzantine Empire against Sasanian Persia in the early 6th century.

2 · Mardin · MuseumMardin Museum

Mardin Museum stands among old Mardin's stone houses in an elegant building erected in the late 19th century as the Syriac Catholic Patriarchate.

3 · Mardin · MuseumSakıp Sabancı Mardin City Museum

The Sakıp Sabancı Mardin City Museum opened in 2009 in a 19th-century former cavalry barracks in Mardin.
