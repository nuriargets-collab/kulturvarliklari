Things to see in Mersin

Museums, archaeological sites, historic buildings and natural sights

Home › Things to see, province by province › Mersin

The Kültür Varlıkları guide lists 44 places in Mersin: 23 archaeological site, 10 museum, 7 castle & historic building, 2 religious building, 2 natural site. Below you will find the highlights, a map and the full list.

⭐ Highlights

1 · Mersin · Archaeological siteAdamkayalar

Adamkayalar ("Men Rocks") is a group of Roman-period human reliefs carved into the sheer cliffs of the Şeytan Deresi canyon between Silifke and Erdemli.

2 · Mersin · Archaeological siteAkkale Archaeological Site

Akkale is the popular name for a Roman and early Byzantine building complex on the seashore at Kumkuyu in Erdemli.

3 · Mersin · Archaeological siteAnemurium Archaeological Site

Anemurium, at Cape Anamur – the southernmost tip of Anatolia – lies between sea and mountains.
