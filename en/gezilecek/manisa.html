The Kültür Varlıkları guide lists 6 places in Manisa: 5 archaeological site, 1 museum. Below you will find the highlights, a map and the full list.

⭐ Highlights

1 · Manisa · Archaeological siteAlaşehir Archaeological Site

Alaşehir preserves remains of ancient Philadelphia, founded by King Attalus II of Pergamon.

2 · Manisa · Archaeological siteBin Tepe Lidya Tümülüsleri

Bin Tepe ("Thousand Mounds") is a vast Lydian burial ground stretching north of Sardis between the Gediz plain and Lake Marmara.
