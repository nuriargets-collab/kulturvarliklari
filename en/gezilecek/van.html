Things to see in Van

Museums, archaeological sites, historic buildings and natural sights

Home › Things to see, province by province › Van

The Kültür Varlıkları guide lists 10 places in Van: 3 castle & historic building, 2 museum, 2 archaeological site, 1 religious building, 1 waterfall, 1 lake. Below you will find the highlights, a map and the full list.

⭐ Highlights

1 · Van · MuseumAkdamar Monument Museum

The Church of the Holy Cross on Akdamar Island, in Lake Van, was built in the early 10th century by King Gagik I of Vaspurakan.

2 · Van · Archaeological siteAyanis Castle

Ayanis Fortress, on a hill by Lake Van north of the city, was built by the Urartian King Rusa II in the 7th century BC.

3 · Van · Archaeological siteMher Kapısı (Meher Kapı)

Meher Kapı (Mher's Door) is a monumental rock niche carved into the slope of Zımzım Tepe in Van's Tuşba district, dating to the Urartian kings Ishpuini and his son…
