Things to see in Adıyaman

Museums, archaeological sites, historic buildings and natural sights

Home › Things to see, province by province › Adıyaman

The Kültür Varlıkları guide lists 8 places in Adıyaman: 6 archaeological site, 1 museum, 1 castle & historic building. Below you will find the highlights, a map and the full list.

⭐ Highlights

1 · Adıyaman · Archaeological siteMount Nemrut

At the summit of Mount Nemrut, about 2,100 metres high, stands the sacred tomb that King Antiochus I of Commagene built for himself in the first century BC.

2 · Adıyaman · MuseumAdıyaman Museum

Adıyaman Museum presents the civilisations that lived along the Euphrates in this region.

3 · Adıyaman · Archaeological siteArsemeia Archaeological Site

Arsameia was a sacred site and summer residence of the Commagene kings, set on a slope above the Kahta valley on the road to Mount Nemrut.
