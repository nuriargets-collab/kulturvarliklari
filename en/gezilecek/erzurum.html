The Kültür Varlıkları guide lists 8 places in Erzurum: 4 museum, 1 castle & historic building, 1 ski resort, 1 waterfall, 1 religious building. Below you will find the highlights, a map and the full list.

⭐ Highlights

1 · Erzurum · MuseumErzurum Atatürk House Museum

Erzurum Atatürk House Museum is the historic house where Mustafa Kemal stayed during the Erzurum Congress of 1919, a turning point in organising the War of Independence…

2 · Erzurum · MuseumErzurum Museum

Erzurum Museum tells the story of communities that lived for millennia on the high plateaus of eastern Anatolia.

3 · Erzurum · MuseumTürk İslam Eserleri and Ethnography Museum

Yakutiye Medrese, built in the early 14th century by Cemaleddin Hoca Yakut in the name of the Ilkhanid ruler, is one of Erzurum's most elegant buildings.
