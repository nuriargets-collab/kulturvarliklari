Things to see in Denizli

Museums, archaeological sites, historic buildings and natural sights

Home › Things to see, province by province › Denizli

The Kültür Varlıkları guide lists 8 places in Denizli: 5 archaeological site, 3 museum. Below you will find the highlights, a map and the full list.

⭐ Highlights

1 · Denizli · Archaeological siteHierapolis (Pamukkale)

Hierapolis sits just above the brilliant white travertines of Pamukkale – one of the rare places where nature and history merge so completely.

2 · Denizli · Archaeological siteLaodikeia Archaeological Site

Laodicea, in the Lycus valley facing the Pamukkale travertines, grew rich in Roman times on banking, textiles and its famous black wool.

3 · Denizli · Archaeological siteBeycesultan Mound

Beycesultan is a large twin-peaked mound about 5 km southwest of Çivril, in a bend of an old branch of the Büyük Menderes (Maeander).
