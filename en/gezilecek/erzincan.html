Things to see in Erzincan

Museums, archaeological sites, historic buildings and natural sights

Home › Things to see, province by province › Erzincan

The Kültür Varlıkları guide lists 6 places in Erzincan: 2 museum, 1 archaeological site, 1 waterfall, 1 canyon, 1 castle & historic building. Below you will find the highlights, a map and the full list.

⭐ Highlights

1 · Erzincan · Archaeological siteAltıntepe Urartu Castle

Altıntepe is an Urartian fortress on a volcanic hill rising 60 metres above the plain at Üzümlü, on the Erzincan–Erzurum road.

2 · Erzincan · MuseumErzincan Museum

Erzincan Museum introduces the past of this fertile plain at the headwaters of the Euphrates.

3 · Erzincan · WaterfallGirlevik Waterfall

Girlevik Waterfall, near Erzincan at the foot of the Munzur Mountains, falls in a series of steps.
