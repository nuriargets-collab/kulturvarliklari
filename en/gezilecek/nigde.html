Things to see in Niğde

Museums, archaeological sites, historic buildings and natural sights

Home › Things to see, province by province › Niğde

The Kültür Varlıkları guide lists 4 places in Niğde: 2 archaeological site, 1 underground city, 1 museum. Below you will find the highlights, a map and the full list.

⭐ Highlights

1 · Niğde · Archaeological siteAncient Tyana and Roman Pool (Kemerhisar)

Tyana, at Kemerhisar in Niğde's Bor district, was known as Tuwanuwa in Hittite times and became one of Cappadocia's major cities under Rome.

2 · Niğde · Underground cityGümüşler Monastery and Underground City

Gümüşler Monastery, in Gümüşler near Niğde, is a rock-cut complex considered one of the best-preserved monasteries of the Cappadocia region.

3 · Niğde · Archaeological siteNiğde Andaval Archaeological Site

Andaval, near Aktaş village in Niğde, holds remains of ancient Andabalis.
