⭐ Highlights

1 · Giresun · MuseumGiresun Museum

Giresun Museum is housed in the restored 19th-century former Gogora Church in a historic seaside quarter; its high ceilings and arched windows give the museum a special…

2 · Giresun · MuseumŞebinkarahisar Atatürk House Museum

The Şebinkarahisar Atatürk House Museum is a traditional local house where Atatürk spent a night during his visit on 11 October 1924.

3 · Giresun · Castle & historic buildingGiresun Castle

Giresun Castle, on the hilltop of the peninsula reaching into the sea north of Giresun, is thought to date from the Kingdom of Pontus in the 2nd century BC.
