Things to see in Şanlıurfa

Museums, archaeological sites, historic buildings and natural sights

Home › Things to see, province by province › Şanlıurfa

The Kültür Varlıkları guide lists 10 places in Şanlıurfa: 5 archaeological site, 2 museum, 2 castle & historic building, 1 religious building. Below you will find the highlights, a map and the full list.

⭐ Highlights

1 · Şanlıurfa · Archaeological siteGöbekli Tepe

Göbeklitepe, north-east of Şanlıurfa, is a Neolithic site about 11,500 years old that changed what we know of human history.

2 · Şanlıurfa · MuseumŞanlıurfa Archaeology Museum

Şanlıurfa Archaeology Museum is one of Türkiye's largest museum complexes and of world importance for its Neolithic collection.

3 · Şanlıurfa · MuseumHaleplibahçe Mosaic Museum

Haleplibahçe Mosaic Museum in Şanlıurfa displays in place the mosaics of a Roman villa found by chance near Balıklıgöl during construction of an amusement park.
