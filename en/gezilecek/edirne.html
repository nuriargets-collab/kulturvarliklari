Things to see in Edirne

Museums, archaeological sites, historic buildings and natural sights

Home › Things to see, province by province › Edirne

The Kültür Varlıkları guide lists 14 places in Edirne: 6 museum, 4 castle & historic building, 2 religious building, 1 archaeological site, 1 hiking trail. Below you will find the highlights, a map and the full list.

⭐ Highlights

1 · Edirne · MuseumEdirne Museum

Edirne Museum introduces Thrace's past from prehistory to the Ottomans.

2 · Edirne · Archaeological siteEdirne Palace Ruins (Saray-ı Cedid)

Edirne Palace (Saray-ı Cedid-i Amire), at Sarayiçi on the Tunca riverbank, was a great Ottoman palace begun under Murad II and enlarged by Mehmed II.

3 · Edirne · MuseumNecmi İğe House Ethnography Museum

Necmi İğe House Ethnography Museum presents Edirne's daily-life culture in a historic house typical of local architecture.
