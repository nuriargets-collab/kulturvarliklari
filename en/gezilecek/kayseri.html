Things to see in Kayseri

Museums, archaeological sites, historic buildings and natural sights

Home › Things to see, province by province › Kayseri

The Kültür Varlıkları guide lists 10 places in Kayseri: 4 museum, 2 archaeological site, 1 ski resort, 1 waterfall, 1 castle & historic building, 1 religious building. Below you will find the highlights, a map and the full list.

⭐ Highlights

1 · Kayseri · MuseumGüpgüpoğlu Mansion Ethnography Museum

Güpgüpoğlu Mansion Ethnography Museum occupies one of the oldest and most impressive surviving mansions in Kayseri.

2 · Kayseri · MuseumKayseri Archaeology Museum

Kayseri Archaeological Museum presents the rich past of this ancient city at the foot of Mount Erciyes.

3 · Kayseri · MuseumKayseri Atatürk House Museum

Kayseri Atatürk House Museum is set in a historic Kayseri house where Atatürk stayed during his visit to the city.
