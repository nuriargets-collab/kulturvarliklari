ℹ️ Practical information

Key information for planning your visit. Prices, hours and rules can change, so always confirm the details with official sources.

🎟 MüzeKart and Museum Pass

Museums and sites run by the Ministry of Culture and Tourism accept the MüzeKart (for residents of Türkiye) and regional Museum Pass options for foreign visitors. Passes can be bought online or at the ticket offices of museums that sell them; on our site, those places are marked "Museum Pass sold here".

Private museums, the National Palaces (e.g. Dolmabahçe), municipal sites (e.g. the Basilica Cistern) and sites in Northern Cyprus generally do not accept the MüzeKart. The "Visiting information" box on each place page tells you which applies.

🕘 Opening hours

Most Ministry sites have different summer and winter hours, closing later in summer. Ticket offices close some time before the site does, so don't leave it too late. Some museums close one day a week, and many are closed on the morning of the first day of religious holidays.

☀️ Best time to visit

  • Spring and autumn are the most comfortable seasons for open-air sites.
  • In summer, visit shadeless sites such as Ephesus, Hierapolis or Göbeklitepe early in the morning; bring a hat, water and good shoes.
  • In Eastern Anatolia and high mountain areas (e.g. Mount Nemrut), snow may restrict access in winter.
  • At popular sites, weekdays and opening time are the quietest.

🕌 Visiting places of worship

  • Mosques may close to visitors at prayer times, churches and synagogues during services.
  • Cover shoulders and knees; in mosques women cover their hair and everyone removes their shoes.
  • Keep quiet, don't photograph people praying and don't use flash.
  • Synagogue visits often require prior permission and ID for security reasons.

📷 Photos and drones

Photography without flash is allowed in many museums but may be banned in some rooms or temporary exhibitions. Tripods and especially drones require permission at archaeological sites. Don't touch the exhibits or climb on ancient structures.
