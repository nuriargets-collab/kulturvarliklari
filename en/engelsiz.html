♿ Accessibility guide

Every place page has an Accessibility section. We only publish information that comes from visitors or museum staff and that we have reviewed – never guesses.

Good to know

  • At sites run by the Ministry of Culture and Tourism, visitors with disabilities and one companion enter free of charge on showing a disability ID or document.
  • Ancient sites often have stone, earth or stepped surfaces, so wheelchair-accessible areas may be limited. We recommend calling the site in advance.
  • Shade is scarce at open-air sites in summer; early mornings are cooler and quieter.
  • Every description on this site can be listened to, and the site is designed to work with keyboards and screen readers.

Share information

If you visited recently, use the "♿ Share accessibility info" button on the place page. Museum staff can also contact us.
