Pamphylia: the ancient cities of Antalya

Routes › Pamphylia: the ancient cities of Antalya

Aspendos with one of the best-preserved Roman theatres, colonnaded Perge and seaside Side. Starting at Antalya Museum, this route links the great ancient cities around Antalya.

🧭 Get directions

Day 1

1 · Antalya · MuseumAntalya Müzesi

Antalya Museum is one of Türkiye's most comprehensive archaeological museums, uniting treasures from Pamphylia, Lycia and Pisidia.

↓ ~1 km (straight line)
2 · Antalya · MuseumAntalya Etnografya Müzesi

Antalya Ethnography Museum presents recent daily life in this region stretching from the Taurus to the coast.

↓ ~24 km (straight line)
3 · Antalya · Archaeological siteTermessos Örenyeri

Termessos, north-west of Antalya, is a Pisidian city built high on the steep slopes of Mount Güllük and almost swallowed by nature.

↓ ~14 km (straight line)
4 · Antalya · Natural siteKarain Mağarası

Karain Cave, north of Antalya at the foot of Katran Mountain, is considered one of Türkiye's most important prehistoric sites.

Day 2

5 · Antalya · Archaeological sitePerge Örenyeri

Perge was one of the grandest cities of the Pamphylian plain and is easy to visit from Antalya.

↓ ~28 km (straight line)
6 · Antalya · Archaeological siteAspendos

Aspendos, on the Köprüçay (ancient Eurymedon), has one of the best-preserved theatres of the ancient world.

↓ ~27 km (straight line)
7 · Antalya · Archaeological siteSide Örenyeri

Side was built on a narrow peninsula and is now a special place where ancient ruins and a modern resort town coexist.

↓ ~0 km (straight line)
8 · Antalya · MuseumSide Müzesi

Side Museum is housed inside a Roman bath building in the heart of ancient Side, so visitors see the finds within the stone walls of an ancient structure.
