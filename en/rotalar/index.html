Routes

Ready-made routes organised day by day to help you plan. Durations are approximate; check opening hours and road conditions before you travel.

Two days in Cappadocia

2 days · 10 stops

Fairy chimneys, rock-cut churches and underground cities: a route fitting Cappadocia's highlights into two days – valleys around Göreme on day one,…

The Lycian coast: Fethiye to Antalya

3 days · 13 stops

Rock tombs, cities of the Lycian League and turquoise bays.

Ionia: Pergamon, Ephesus and Miletus

3 days · 13 stops

An Aegean route linking some of antiquity's most brilliant cities – from Pergamon in the north, via Ephesus, to Priene, Miletus and Didyma in the…

Caria: around Bodrum and Milas

3 days · 12 stops

From Halicarnassus, city of the Mausoleum, to the mountain sanctuary of Labraunda – a route through Caria's lesser-known treasures.

Pamphylia: the ancient cities of Antalya

2 days · 8 stops

Aspendos with one of the best-preserved Roman theatres, colonnaded Perge and seaside Side.

Göbeklitepe and Upper Mesopotamia

4 days · 11 stops

A four-day journey from humanity's oldest monumental structures to Nemrut's colossal statues, Harran's beehive houses and Mardin's stone lanes.

Hittites and Phrygians

3 days · 8 stops

From Hattusa, capital of Anatolia's first great empire, to King Midas's Gordion – with the unique collection of the Museum of Anatolian Civilisations.

Lake Van and Urartu

2 days · 7 stops

Around Lake Van: from Tushpa, capital of Urartu, to Akdamar Island and the Seljuk tombstones of Ahlat.

Istanbul: historic peninsula and Bosphorus

2 days · 9 stops

Two days through the layers of the Byzantine and Ottoman capital: museums around Sultanahmet, then Galata and Rumeli Fortress on the Bosphorus.

In Atatürk's footsteps: Samsun to Ankara

4 days · 5 stops

Following the national struggle from its start in Samsun in 1919, via the Erzurum and Sivas congresses, to the first parliament buildings in Ankara.

Two days in Northern Cyprus

2 days · 15 stops

Day one: Selimiye, Büyük Han and the Mevlevi Tekke in walled Nicosia, then over to Kyrenia for Bellapais Abbey, St Hilarion Castle and Kyrenia…

Troy and Çanakkale

2 days · 10 stops

Day one starts at the site of Troy and the Troy Museum beside it, then continues via Alexandria Troas and the Smintheion to reach Assos by sunset.

Eastern Black Sea: from Sumela to Rize

2 days · 7 stops

Day one: walk up to Sumela Monastery in the Altındere Valley early in the morning, then the museum in the Kostaki Mansion and Little Ayvasıl Church…

Diyarbakır, Hasankeyf and Mardin

2 days · 7 stops

Day one in Diyarbakır: the Archaeology Museum and the houses of Cahit Sıtkı Tarancı and Ziya Gökalp, then the Batman Museum in the late afternoon.
