Early Christian trails from Antakya to Silifke

Routes › Early Christian trails from Antakya to Silifke

Day one in Antakya: the Church of St Peter and the Hatay Archaeology Museum, home to one of the world's richest mosaic collections, then St Simeon Monastery and Seleucia Pieria (Çevlik). Day two follows St Paul in Tarsus, the sanctuary of St Thecla at Silifke and seaside Kızkalesi. As the region was affected by the 2023 earthquakes, check what is open before you go.

🧭 Get directions

Day 1

1 · Hatay · MuseumSt. Pierre Anıt Müzesi

The Church of St Peter is a cave church carved into the slope of Mount Staurin on the edge of Antakya and is regarded as one of the oldest Christian places of worship.

↓ ~2 km (straight line)
2 · Hatay · MuseumHatay Arkeoloji Müzesi

Hatay Archaeological Museum is famous for one of the world's richest mosaic collections.

↓ ~17 km (straight line)
3 · Hatay · Religious buildingSt. Simon Manastırı

The Monastery of St Simeon, on a high mountain near Samandağ overlooking Antakya and the Orontes valley, was founded in memory of Simeon the Younger, who lived in the…

↓ ~11 km (straight line)
4 · Hatay · Archaeological siteÇevlik Örenyeri

Çevlik, in Samandağ, holds the remains of Seleucia Pieria, founded as the port of ancient Antioch.

Day 2

5 · Mersin · Religious buildingTarsus St. Paul Kuyusu

St Paul's Well, in historic Tarsus, is an ancient well in an area believed to belong to the house where the apostle Paul was born.

↓ ~0 km (straight line)
6 · Mersin · MuseumTarsus St. Paul Anıt Müzesi

St Paul's Memorial Museum in Tarsus is a 19th-century church dedicated to the apostle Paul.

↓ ~0 km (straight line)
7 · Mersin · MuseumMersin Tarsus Müzesi

Tarsus Museum presents the rich heritage of Tarsus, with thousands of years of continuous settlement.

↓ ~106 km (straight line)
8 · Mersin · Archaeological siteSilifke Ayatekla Örenyeri

Ayatekla, just south of Silifke, is an important pilgrimage site regarded as where St Thecla, said to be one of the first followers of the apostle Paul, lived and died.

↓ ~22 km (straight line)
9 · Mersin · Castle & historic buildingMersin Kız Kalesi

Kızkalesi (Maiden's Castle), at Erdemli a few hundred metres offshore, is an enchanting sea castle covering an entire islet.

↓ ~3 km (straight line)
10 · Mersin · MuseumSilifke Narlıkuyu Mozaik Müzesi

Narlıkuyu Mosaic Museum, in the cove of Narlıkuyu near Silifke, protects in place the mosaic floor of a 4th-century Roman bath.
