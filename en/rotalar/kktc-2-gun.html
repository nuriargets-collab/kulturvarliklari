Two days in Northern Cyprus

Routes › Two days in Northern Cyprus

Day one: Selimiye, Büyük Han and the Mevlevi Tekke in walled Nicosia, then over to Kyrenia for Bellapais Abbey, St Hilarion Castle and Kyrenia Castle at sunset. Day two is for Famagusta: Salamis, the Royal Tombs, St Barnabas and Enkomi, then Othello Castle and Lala Mustafa Pasha Mosque in the walled city.

🧭 Get directions

Day 1

1 · Lefkoşa · Religious buildingSelimiye Camii

Selimiye Mosque rises at the heart of the walled old city of Lefkoşa (Nicosia).

↓ ~0 km (straight line)
2 · Lefkoşa · MuseumLapidari Müzesi

The Lapidary Museum, right beside Selimiye Mosque in Lefkoşa, occupies a two-storey 15th-century stone building.

↓ ~0 km (straight line)
3 · Lefkoşa · Castle & historic buildingBüyük Han

Büyük Han ('Great Inn'), in old Lefkoşa, is a two-storey caravanserai built in 1572 by Lala Mustafa Pasha, the island's first Ottoman governor.

↓ ~0 km (straight line)
4 · Lefkoşa · MuseumMevlevi Tekke Müzesi

The Mevlevi Tekke Museum, near the Kyrenia Gate in Lefkoşa, occupies the buildings of a Mevlevi lodge founded in the 17th century.

↓ ~1 km (straight line)
5 · Lefkoşa · MuseumDerviş Paşa Konağı

Dervish Pasha Mansion, in the Arabahmet quarter of Lefkoşa, is a two-storey stone-and-timber Ottoman house of the 19th century.

↓ ~15 km (straight line)
6 · Girne · Religious buildingBellapais Manastırı

Bellapais Abbey sits on a terrace overlooking the sea in Beylerbeyi village, south-east of Girne, at the foot of the Beşparmak mountains.

↓ ~7 km (straight line)
7 · Girne · Castle & historic buildingSt. Hilarion Kalesi

↓ ~5 km (straight line)
8 · Girne · Castle & historic buildingGirne Kalesi ve Batık Gemi Müzesi

Kyrenia Castle rises at the eastern end of Girne's (Kyrenia's) historic harbour.

Day 2

9 · Gazimağusa · Archaeological siteSalamis Örenyeri

Salamis, about 8 km north of Gazimağusa (Famagusta) among dunes and pines by the sea, is one of Cyprus's largest ancient cities.

↓ ~2 km (straight line)
10 · Gazimağusa · Archaeological siteKral Mezarları Müzesi ve Örenyeri

The Royal Tombs, west of ancient Salamis, are the city's elite necropolis of the 8th–7th centuries BC.

↓ ~1 km (straight line)
11 · Gazimağusa · Religious buildingSt. Barnabas Manastırı

St Barnabas Monastery, near Salamis, bears the name of the Apostle Barnabas, regarded as founder of the Church of Cyprus.

↓ ~1 km (straight line)
12 · Gazimağusa · Archaeological siteEnkomi (Alasya) Örenyeri

Enkomi (Alasia), near Tuzla village, holds the remains of one of the most important Late Bronze Age cities on Cyprus.

↓ ~7 km (straight line)
13 · Gazimağusa · Castle & historic buildingOthello Kalesi

Othello Castle is a citadel built to guard the harbour in the walled Kaleiçi quarter of Gazimağusa (Famagusta).

↓ ~0 km (straight line)
14 · Gazimağusa · Religious buildingLala Mustafa Paşa Camii

Lala Mustafa Pasha Mosque is the emblem of Gazimağusa's walled city.

↓ ~0 km (straight line)
