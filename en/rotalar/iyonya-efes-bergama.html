Ionia: Pergamon, Ephesus and Miletus

Routes › Ionia: Pergamon, Ephesus and Miletus

An Aegean route linking some of antiquity's most brilliant cities – from Pergamon in the north, via Ephesus, to Priene, Miletus and Didyma in the Meander valley.

2 · İzmir · Archaeological siteBergama Asklepion Örenyeri

The Asclepieion of Pergamon, dedicated to the healing god Asclepius, was one of the most famous therapeutic centres of the ancient world.

↓ ~1 km (straight line)
3 · İzmir · Archaeological siteBergama Kızılavlu

The Red Basilica in central Bergama is a huge Roman temple named after its red brick.

Bergama Museum is one of Türkiye's oldest museums and displays the rich heritage of ancient Pergamon.
