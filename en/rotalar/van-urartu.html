Around Lake Van: from Tushpa, capital of Urartu, to Akdamar Island and the Seljuk tombstones of Ahlat.

Van Museum holds one of the world's richest collections of the Urartian civilisation.

The Church of the Holy Cross on Akdamar Island, in Lake Van, was built in the early 10th century by King Gagik I of Vaspurakan.

↓ ~67 km (straight line)
6 · Bitlis · Archaeological siteAhlat Selçuklu Mezarlığı

The Ahlat Seljuk Cemetery on Lake Van is regarded as one of the largest and most impressive cemeteries of the Turkish-Islamic world.

Ahlat Museum presents the rich heritage of historic Ahlat on the north-west shore of Lake Van.
