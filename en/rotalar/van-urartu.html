Lake Van and Urartu

Routes › Lake Van and Urartu

Around Lake Van: from Tushpa, capital of Urartu, to Akdamar Island and the Seljuk tombstones of Ahlat.

🧭 Get directions

Day 1

1 · Van · Castle & historic buildingVan Castle

Van Fortress rises on a long, sheer rock beside Lake Van.

↓ ~0 km (straight line)
2 · Van · MuseumVan Museum

Van Museum holds one of the world's richest collections of the Urartian civilisation.

↓ ~20 km (straight line)
3 · Van · Castle & historic buildingÇavuştepe Castle

Çavuştepe Castle, south-east of Van, is an Urartian fortress on a long, narrow ridge above the Gürpınar plain.

↓ ~29 km (straight line)
4 · Van · Castle & historic buildingHoşap Castle

Hoşap Castle, in Gürpınar, Van, crowns a rocky hill beside the Hoşap stream.

Day 2

5 · Van · MuseumAkdamar Monument Museum

The Church of the Holy Cross on Akdamar Island, in Lake Van, was built in the early 10th century by King Gagik I of Vaspurakan.

↓ ~67 km (straight line)
6 · Bitlis · Archaeological siteAhlat Seljuk Cemetery

The Ahlat Seljuk Cemetery on Lake Van is regarded as one of the largest and most impressive cemeteries of the Turkish-Islamic world.

↓ ~0 km (straight line)
7 · Bitlis · MuseumAhlat Museum

Ahlat Museum presents the rich heritage of historic Ahlat on the north-west shore of Lake Van.
