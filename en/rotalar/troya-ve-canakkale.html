Troy and Çanakkale

Routes › Troy and Çanakkale

Day one starts at the site of Troy and the Troy Museum beside it, then continues via Alexandria Troas and the Smintheion to reach Assos by sunset. Day two follows the Gallipoli campaign through the batteries on both shores of the Dardanelles and Kilitbahir Fortress.

Day 1

1 · Çanakkale · Archaeological siteTroya

Troy became the most famous city in world literature through Homer's Iliad.

3 · Çanakkale · Archaeological siteAlexandria Troas Örenyeri

↓ ~25 km (straight line)
4 · Çanakkale · Archaeological siteApollon Smintheion Örenyeri

Apollo Smintheion, in the village of Gülpınar, is a sanctuary of Apollo 'Smintheus', associated with mice and plague.

↓ ~19 km (straight line)
5 · Çanakkale · Archaeological siteAssos Örenyeri

Assos, above the village of Behramkale, crowns a volcanic hill overlooking the Gulf of Edremit and Lesbos.

Day 2

6 · Çanakkale · MuseumKilitbahir Kale Müzesi

Kilitbahir Castle, on the European shore at the narrowest point of the Dardanelles, was built in the 15th century by Sultan Mehmed the Conqueror.

↓ ~2 km (straight line)
7 · Çanakkale · MuseumNamazgâh Tabyası Müzesi

Namazgâh Bastion at Kilitbahir is one of the key links in the coastal defence line on the European side of the Dardanelles.

↓ ~3 km (straight line)
8 · Çanakkale · MuseumHamidiye Tabyası ve Çanakkale Savaşları Tarihi Müzesi

Hamidiye Bastion and the Museum of the History of the Çanakkale Wars occupy one of the bastions that played a key role in defending the Dardanelles.

↓ ~13 km (straight line)
9 · Çanakkale · MuseumÇanakkale Destanı Tanıtım Merkezi

The Çanakkale Epic Promotion Centre on the Gallipoli peninsula presents the 1915 Gallipoli campaign with modern interpretive techniques.
