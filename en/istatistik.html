📊 Türkiye Museum and Archaeological Site Statistics 2025

Museums and archaeological sites in Türkiye reached 65.9 million visits in 2025. Up-to-date figures from official data, with charts and the most visited places.

More figures

  • 220 museums belong to the Ministry, 426 are private and 18 belong to the National Palaces.
  • 60.4% of objects in Ministry museums are coins.
  • Revenue of Ministry museums and sites exceeded TRY 7.3 billion in 2025.
