18 March Çanakkale Victory and 25 April Anzac commemorations

18 March · 25 April · Gallipoli Peninsula, Çanakkale

On the anniversaries of the Gallipoli campaign, ceremonies are held in the Gallipoli Peninsula Historical Area; thousands from Australia and New Zealand attend the 25 April dawn service.

Related place: Hamidiye Tabyası and Çanakkale Savaşları Tarihi Museum

Dates may change each year; check the organiser's or the Ministry's official announcements before you go.
