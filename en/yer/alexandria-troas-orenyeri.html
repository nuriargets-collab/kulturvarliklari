Alexandria Troas Örenyeri

Home › Marmara › Çanakkale
Location Ezine, ÇanakkaleRegion MarmaraType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Alexandria Troas, near Dalyan village by Ezine on the Aegean coast, was a large port founded in the Hellenistic period by one of Alexander the Great's generals and later named in Alexander's memory. Under Rome it became one of the region's chief harbours. Among ruins spread across oak-covered land, the high walls of the great Roman bath complex stand out; the ancient harbour basin, walls and theatre can also be seen. The apostle Paul is said to have passed through. A quiet stop between Troy and Assos.
