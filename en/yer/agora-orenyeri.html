Agora Örenyeri

The Agora of Smyrna, in the very centre of modern İzmir, was the heart of the ancient city's public life. The city was refounded after Alexander the Great at the foot of Kadifekale (Mount Pagos); the agora, destroyed by a great earthquake in the 2nd century AD, was rebuilt under Marcus Aurelius. Visitors can walk the cool corridors of the basilica's surviving vaulted basement, where ancient graffiti – scratched texts and drawings – preserve intimate traces of daily life, and water still flows through channels below. Close to Kemeraltı Bazaar.
