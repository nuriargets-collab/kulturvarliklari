Ani, east of Kars on the edge of the deep Arpaçay gorge right at today's border with Armenia, is a captivating medieval city. Capital of the Bagratid Armenian kingdom in the 10th–11th centuries, it was called the 'city of 1001 churches'. Taken in 1064 by the Seljuk Sultan Alp Arslan, it also holds the Ebul Menuçehr Mosque, considered one of the first Turkish mosques in Anatolia. A cathedral, churches, palace ruins and walls scattered across the steppe show the wealth of this Silk Road city. UNESCO World Heritage since 2016.
