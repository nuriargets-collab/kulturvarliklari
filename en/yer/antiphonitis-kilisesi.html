Antiphonitis Church

Antiphonitis Kilisesi

Antiphonitis Church lies in a remote, wooded valley on the northern slopes of the Beşparmak mountains near Esentepe. Built in the 12th century, its large dome rests on eight supports, a plan rarely seen on Cyprus. A Lusignan-era portico and 15th-century frescoes add layers of history; the Last Judgement and the Tree of Jesse are the best known. Though badly damaged over time, the frescoes are being protected through conservation work. Its peaceful setting makes it a calm stop amid nature.
