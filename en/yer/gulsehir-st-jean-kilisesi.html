Gülşehir-St.Jean Kilisesi

Home › Central Anatolia › Nevşehir
Location Gülşehir, NevşehirRegion Central AnatoliaType Religious buildingPeriod Bizans (13. yy)
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Church of St John (Karşı Kilise) at the entrance to Gülşehir is a two-storey rock church. Its upper church bears very well-preserved 13th-century frescoes, showing that Christian communities continued to worship here in the Seljuk era; scenes of the Last Judgement, rare in Cappadocia, stand out. Restored and open to visitors, it lies just off the main Cappadocia route.
