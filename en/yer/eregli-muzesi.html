Ereğli Müzesi

Home › Central Anatolia › Konya
Location Ereğli, KonyaRegion Central AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Ereğli Museum presents the rich past of Ereğli and the northern foothills of the Taurus. Nearby at İvriz is a great rock relief, one of the most famous Neo-Hittite works, showing King Warpalawas of Tuwana praying before the storm and fertility god Tarhunza, whose grapes and ears of wheat symbolise the region's agricultural wealth. Mound finds, Roman sculpture, coins and ethnography are displayed; best visited with the İvriz relief.
