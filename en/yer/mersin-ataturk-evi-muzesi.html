Mersin Atatürk Evi Müzesi

Home › Mediterranean › Mersin
Location Akdeniz, MersinRegion MediterraneanType MuseumPeriod Cumhuriyet
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Mersin Atatürk House Museum is a late 19th-century mansion where Atatürk stayed on visits to Mersin. It reflects Mediterranean coastal architecture with Levantine influences from the period when Mersin grew rapidly as a port. Photographs, documents and period-furnished rooms recall his stays.
