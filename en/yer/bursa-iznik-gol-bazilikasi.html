Bursa İznik Göl Bazilikası

The İznik Lake Basilica is an early Christian church lying in the shallow waters of Lake İznik, a short distance from the shore, which made headlines in 2014 through aerial photographs. The remains are thought to belong to a basilica built over the place where Saint Neophytos is said to have been martyred in Roman times. Later destroyed by earthquakes, it sank beneath the lake. Underwater research has revealed its plan and building phases. Check with the local museum directorate for current viewing arrangements.
