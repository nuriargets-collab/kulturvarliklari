Yesemek Örenyeri

Home › Southeastern Anatolia › Gaziantep
Location İslahiye, GaziantepRegion Southeastern AnatoliaType Archaeological sitePeriod Geç Hitit
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Yesemek, in İslahiye, is a unique site from the Hittite era regarded as one of the world's largest open-air quarries and sculpture workshops. Hundreds of unfinished statues – lions, sphinxes, mountain gods and relief blocks – lie scattered on the hillside, some just cut from the rock, others nearly complete. They let visitors follow step by step how Hittite and Neo-Hittite sculptors worked and how stone travelled from quarry to palace gate. A silent sculpture garden that fascinates history lovers and photographers alike.
