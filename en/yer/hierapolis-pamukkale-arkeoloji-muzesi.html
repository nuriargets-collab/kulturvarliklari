Hierapolis (Pamukkale) Arkeoloji Müzesi

Home › Aegean › Denizli
Location Pamukkale, DenizliRegion AegeanType MuseumPeriod Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Hierapolis Archaeological Museum is housed in the city's great Roman bath building, whose high vaulted halls make an impressive setting. Statues, sarcophagi, inscriptions and small finds from Hierapolis, Laodicea, Tripolis and nearby cities are displayed. Reliefs from the theatre's stage building, telling the myths of Apollo and Artemis, are outstanding examples of Roman stone carving. An ideal cool break during a visit to the travertines and the ruins.
