Erzurum Atatürk Evi Müzesi

Home › Eastern Anatolia › Erzurum
Location Yakutiye, ErzurumRegion Eastern AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Cumhuriyet
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Erzurum Atatürk House Museum is the historic house where Mustafa Kemal stayed during the Erzurum Congress of 1919, a turning point in organising the War of Independence whose decisions set out the principles of the national struggle. The two-storey traditional Erzurum house, with thick walls built for harsh winters and a wooden interior, displays the room he used, his desk, documents and photographs.
