Kadıkalesi Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › Aydın
Location Kuşadası, AydınRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Bizans / Ortaçağ
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Kadıkalesi, on a hill overlooking the sea near Kuşadası, is a multi-layered site associated with ancient Anaia. It was used from antiquity through Byzantine and medieval times; its walls show its role as a coastal fortress, especially in the Byzantine period. Ongoing excavations of church remains, dwellings and pottery shed light on the area's medieval trade and religious life. A lesser-known stop near Kuşadası and Ephesus.
