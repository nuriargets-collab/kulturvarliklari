Çatalhöyük Örenyeri

Home › Central Anatolia › Konya

🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Çatalhöyük, on the Konya plain, is a great Neolithic settlement and one of the most important witnesses to humanity's transition to settled, town-like life. Some nine thousand years ago thousands of people are thought to have lived here without streets: mud-brick houses stood wall to wall and were entered by ladder from the roofs. Walls were painted with hunting scenes and patterns, some rooms decorated with bull heads and horns, and the dead buried beneath the floors. Excavation areas under protective roofs and reconstructed houses help visitors imagine this world. UNESCO World Heritage since 2012.
