Bitlis Etnografya Müzesi

Home › Eastern Anatolia › Bitlis
Location Merkez, BitlisRegion Eastern AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Osmanlı / Yakın Dönem
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Bitlis Ethnography Museum presents the daily-life culture of Bitlis, a city built in a narrow valley and known for its stone houses and castle. For centuries it lay on an important pass between eastern Anatolia and Mesopotamia, which gave it a rich craft and trade tradition. Local costumes, weavings, kitchenware, weapons and crafts recreate recent life; afterwards a walk among cut-stone mansions up to the castle reveals the city's distinctive character.
