Osman Hamdi Bey Müzesi

Home › Marmara › Kocaeli
Location Gebze, KocaeliRegion MarmaraType MuseumPeriod Osmanlı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Osman Hamdi Bey Museum is in the village of Eskihisar, Gebze, in the house where the painter, archaeologist and museum founder spent his summers and created some of his works. Osman Hamdi Bey founded the Istanbul Archaeology Museums and the School of Fine Arts and was a pioneer of Turkish painting, known for works such as 'The Tortoise Trainer'. The seaside house with its garden reflects his working environment; reproductions, belongings and photographs are displayed.
