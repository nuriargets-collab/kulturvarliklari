Karanlık Kilise (Göreme)

Home › Central Anatolia › Nevşehir
Location Göreme, NevşehirRegion Central AnatoliaType Religious buildingPeriod Bizans (11. yy)UNESCO 1985
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Dark Church, within Göreme Open-Air Museum, has Cappadocia's best-preserved frescoes. Named for the single small window admitting light, its darkness ironically shielded the paintings from fading, keeping their colours vivid. The 11th-century scenes – Nativity, Baptism, Last Supper, Crucifixion – stand out in deep blues. Opened after long restoration, it requires a separate ticket.
