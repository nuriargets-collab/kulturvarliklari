Gaziantep Archaeological Museum presents thousands of years of history of Gaziantep and its surroundings, a transition zone between Mesopotamia and Anatolia. Densely settled since prehistory, the region hosted important centres of Hittite and Neo-Hittite kingdoms, Assyrians, Hellenistic kingdoms and Rome. Stone tools, pottery, Neo-Hittite reliefs, Roman sculpture and gravestones, coins and jewellery form a wide collection – the other face of Gaziantep's archaeology beside the Zeugma Mosaic Museum.
