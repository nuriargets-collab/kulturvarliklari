Pessinus Örenyeri

Home › Central Anatolia › Eskişehir
Location Sivrihisar, EskişehirRegion Central AnatoliaType Archaeological sitePeriod Frig / Helenistik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Pessinus, in the village of Ballıhisar near Sivrihisar, was one of the most important cult centres of the Mother Goddess Cybele in the ancient world. According to ancient sources, in the late 3rd century BC, during Rome's war with Carthage, the sacred stone representing Cybele was taken from Pessinus to Rome, where she was worshipped as Magna Mater. Visitors see the temple area, remains of a monumental canal and ancient stones reused in village houses.
