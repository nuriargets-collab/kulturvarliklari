Cahit Sıtkı Tarancı Evi Etnografya Müzesi

Home › Southeastern Anatolia › Diyarbakır
Location Sur, DiyarbakırRegion Southeastern AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Osmanlı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Cahit Sıtkı Tarancı Museum is the traditional house in Diyarbakır's historic Sur district where the poet, loved for generations for his poem 'Thirty-Five Years', was born. With black basalt walls, an inner courtyard, an iwan and summer and winter sections, it is a fine example of local architecture adapted to the hot climate. Documents, photographs and personal belongings of the poet sit alongside ethnographic room settings.
