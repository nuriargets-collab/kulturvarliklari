Kanlı Divane Örenyeri

Home › Mediterranean › Mersin
Location Erdemli, MersinRegion MediterraneanType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma / Bizans
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Kanlıdivane, ancient Kanytelis near Erdemli, is a striking city built around a vast sinkhole lined with ancient buildings. Rock-cut reliefs can be seen on the pit's steep walls; its local name, meaning 'bloody', is said to come from legends about the pit. Several Byzantine churches, a Hellenistic tower, cisterns and houses surround it, with tomb monuments and rock tombs nearby – one of Cilicia's most unusual settlements.
