Hamidiye Tabyası ve Çanakkale Savaşları Tarihi Müzesi

Home › Marmara › Çanakkale
Location Merkez, ÇanakkaleRegion MarmaraType MuseumPeriod Osmanlı / Birinci Dünya Savaşı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Hamidiye Bastion and the Museum of the History of the Çanakkale Wars occupy one of the bastions that played a key role in defending the Dardanelles. Near the strait's narrowest point, these bastions were vital in the naval defence of 18 March 1915 against the Allied fleet's attempt to force the passage. Gun emplacements behind earthworks, magazines and galleries explain coastal defence of the time, and documents, photographs, weapons and soldiers' belongings are displayed.
