Rize Müzesi

Home › Black Sea › Rize
Location Merkez, RizeRegion Black SeaType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli

Rize Museum presents the heritage of Rize, known for green valleys and tea gardens. Besides archaeological finds, ethnography dominates: wooden household items, costumes, weavings, copperware and instruments such as the tulum bagpipe, reflecting life between mountains and sea; traditional wooden granaries (serender) show the ingenuity of Black Sea architecture.
