Derinkuyu Yeraltı Şehri

Home › Central Anatolia › Nevşehir
Location Derinkuyu, NevşehirRegion Central AnatoliaType Underground cityPeriod BizansUNESCO 1985
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Derinkuyu Underground City is one of Cappadocia's deepest and largest. Its origins are debated, but it was extensively enlarged in Byzantine times as a refuge during Arab raids. Descending many levels into the soft volcanic tuff are stables, wine and oil presses, kitchens, churches, ventilation shafts and wells; huge wheel-shaped rolling stones sealed the passages. The narrow, low corridors are unforgettable – but not for the claustrophobic. Part of the UNESCO-listed Cappadocia site (1985).
