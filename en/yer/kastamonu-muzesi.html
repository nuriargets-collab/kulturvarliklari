Kastamonu Museum presents the past of the region known in antiquity as Paphlagonia. Settled since prehistory, it saw important Roman cities such as Pompeiopolis and a bright medieval period under the Çobanids and Candarids. Sculpture, mosaics, gravestones, coins and small finds are displayed alongside works on Kastamonu's historic and cultural heritage.
