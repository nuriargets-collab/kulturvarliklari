Silifke Atatürk Evi ve Etnografya Müzesi

Home › Mediterranean › Mersin
Location Silifke, MersinRegion MediterraneanType MuseumPeriod Cumhuriyet
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Silifke Atatürk House and Ethnography Museum, in a historic building, recalls Atatürk's visit to Silifke with photographs and documents, while other rooms display costumes, weavings, household items and crafts of people living between the Göksu delta and the Taurus, many reflecting Yörük nomadic culture.
