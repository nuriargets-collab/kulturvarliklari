Adam Mickiewicz Müzesi

Home › Marmara › İstanbul
Location Beyoğlu, İstanbulRegion MarmaraType MuseumPeriod Osmanlı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Adam Mickiewicz Museum occupies the Istanbul house where Poland's national poet spent his last days. Mickiewicz came to Istanbul in the mid-19th century during the Crimean War to support the formation of a Polish unit on Ottoman soil, and died here. The house in Tarlabaşı now keeps his memory alive with documents, photographs and belongings about his life, work and Turkish-Polish friendship – a friendship rooted in the Ottoman refusal to recognise the partition of Poland.
