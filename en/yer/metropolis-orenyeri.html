Metropolis Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › İzmir
Location Torbalı, İzmirRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Metropolis, near Torbalı on a hill overlooking the Küçük Menderes plain, means 'city of the Mother Goddess', recalling the millennia-long cult of the mother goddess in the region. Its most striking building is an elegant Hellenistic theatre whose marble seats of honour in the front rows bear lion-paw decoration. Baths, a council house, a stoa and houses have been uncovered by long-running excavations – a quiet alternative to busy Ephesus.
