Niğde Andaval Örenyeri

Home › Central Anatolia › Niğde
Location Merkez, NiğdeRegion Central AnatoliaType Archaeological sitePeriod Roma / Bizans
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Andaval, near Aktaş village in Niğde, holds remains of ancient Andabalis. Its main monument is the Church of Constantine and Helena, traditionally said to have been commissioned by Helena, mother of Emperor Constantine; only parts of its walls and traces of frescoes survive. The area was known in antiquity for horse breeding – a quiet stop for lesser-known Christian heritage.
