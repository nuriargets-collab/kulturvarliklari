Aydın Arkeoloji Müzesi

Home › Aegean › Aydın
Location Efeler, AydınRegion AegeanType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli

Aydın Archaeological Museum gathers works from the ancient cities of the fertile Büyük Menderes valley. Aydın's ancient name was Tralleis, birthplace of Anthemius, one of the architects of Hagia Sophia. Sculpture, portraits, architectural pieces, sarcophagi, pottery and coins come from Tralleis, Magnesia, Nysa and other sites; marble works show the skill of local workshops. A good starting point before exploring the valley's sites.
