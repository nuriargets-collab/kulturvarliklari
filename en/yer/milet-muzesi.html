Milet Müzesi

Home › Aegean › Aydın
Location Didim, AydınRegion AegeanType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Miletus Museum, next to ancient Miletus, displays finds from the city and nearby sanctuaries. The sacred way between Miletus and Didyma, the city's colonies, trade links and philosophical tradition are its main themes. Sculpture, architectural pieces, inscriptions, pottery and coins reveal Miletus's wealth from the Archaic period to Roman and Byzantine times. Visiting before the ruins helps place the theatre, agora and harbours in context.
