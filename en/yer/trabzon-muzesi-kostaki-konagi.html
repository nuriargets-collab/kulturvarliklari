Trabzon Müzesi (Kostaki Konağı)

Home › Black Sea › Trabzon
Location Ortahisar, TrabzonRegion Black SeaType MuseumPeriod 20. yy başı / Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Trabzon Museum is housed in a grand European-style mansion built in the early 20th century for the Greek banker Kostaki Theophylaktos. Materials said to have come from Italy, painted ceilings, woodwork and furniture make the building itself a highlight. Archaeological finds from ancient Trapezus, the Empire of Trebizond and Ottoman times are shown with ethnography.
