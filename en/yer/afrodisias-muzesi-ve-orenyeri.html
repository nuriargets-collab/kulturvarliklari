Afrodisias Müzesi ve Örenyeri

Aphrodisias, named after Aphrodite, goddess of love and beauty, was renowned across the Roman Mediterranean for its art. Nearby white-marble quarries fostered a strong school of sculpture whose signed works reached Rome. The monumental Tetrapylon gate is the city's emblem; one of the ancient world's best-preserved stadiums, the relief-covered Sebasteion, the theatre and the Temple of Aphrodite show its splendour. Excavations led for many years by Prof. Kenan Erim made it world-famous. The site museum's portraits and Sebasteion reliefs are superb. UNESCO World Heritage since 2017.
