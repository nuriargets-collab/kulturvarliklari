Laodikeia Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › Denizli
Location Pamukkale, DenizliRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Laodicea, in the Lycus valley facing the Pamukkale travertines, grew rich in Roman times on banking, textiles and its famous black wool. It is also one of the seven churches named in the Book of Revelation. Extensive recent excavation and restoration have made it one of Anatolia's most impressive sites: Syria Street with re-erected columns, two theatres, a stadium, temples, fountains and early churches make visitors feel they are walking through a living ancient city.
