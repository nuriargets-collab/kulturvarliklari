Eti Arkeoloji Müzesi

Eti Archaeology Museum in Eskişehir was established by a private institution and displays archaeological works from various periods of Anatolian history. Private museums, operating under ministry supervision, contribute to promoting Türkiye's heritage. Pottery, glass, coins and small finds from prehistory to Roman, Byzantine and later periods are presented chronologically – a stop to add to a cultural tour of Odunpazarı.
