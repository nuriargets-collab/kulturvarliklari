Aziz (St.) Nikolaos Anıt Müzesi

Home › Mediterranean › Antalya
Location Demre, AntalyaRegion MediterraneanType MuseumPeriod Bizans
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The St. Nicholas Memorial Museum in Demre is the church named after Saint Nicholas, the inspiration behind the figure of Santa Claus. Nicholas was bishop of the Lycian city of Myra in the 4th century; stories of his generosity, especially his secret help to poor families, made him a beloved saint. Destroyed and rebuilt many times, the church shows layers from different periods, with frescoes and mosaic floors. A sarcophagus attributed to the saint draws particular interest. Best combined with Myra and the harbour of Andriake.
