Denizli City Museum aims to tell the city's story from its beginnings to today through the memory of its people. Heir to great ancient cities like Laodicea and Hierapolis, Denizli has also been shaped since the Middle Ages by weaving and trade. Old photographs, documents, crafts, tools of trades and everyday objects trace its transformation, with symbols such as the famous Denizli rooster. A chance to know Denizli as a city with its own character.
