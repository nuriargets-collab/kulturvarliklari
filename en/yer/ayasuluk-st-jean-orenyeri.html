Ayasuluk - St.Jean Örenyeri

The Basilica of St John, on the slope of Ayasuluk Hill in Selçuk, was built in the 6th century by Emperor Justinian over the place where tradition locates the tomb of John the Apostle. This vast cross-shaped, multi-domed basilica was one of the largest churches of its time and a major medieval pilgrimage site. Re-erected columns, marble floors and its baptistery convey its former grandeur, and the presumed tomb is marked at its centre. The view from its terrace towards the İsa Bey Mosque and the Temple of Artemis is a fine finale to a visit to Ephesus.
