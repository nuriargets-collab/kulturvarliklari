Aksaray Müzesi

Aksaray Museum brings together thousands of years of history of Aksaray, the western gateway to Cappadocia. The region holds one of the world's oldest villages at Aşıklı Höyük alongside Byzantine rock churches and Seljuk caravanserais, and the museum presents this richness from prehistory to Ottoman times. Mummies from the Byzantine period are among its most intriguing exhibits. A good stop before visiting Ihlara Valley.
