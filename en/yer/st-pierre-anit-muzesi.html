St. Pierre Anıt Müzesi

Home › Mediterranean › Hatay
Location Antakya, HatayRegion MediterraneanType MuseumPeriod Erken Hristiyanlık
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Church of St Peter is a cave church carved into the slope of Mount Staurin on the edge of Antakya and is regarded as one of the oldest Christian places of worship. Ancient Antioch was a key centre of the early Christian community, and tradition holds that Jesus's followers were first called 'Christians' here. It is dedicated to the apostle Peter, believed to have preached here; the stone façade was added much later, in the Crusader period. Check current status before visiting.
