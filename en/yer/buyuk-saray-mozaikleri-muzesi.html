Büyük Saray Mozaikleri Müzesi

Home › Marmara › İstanbul
Location Fatih, İstanbulRegion MarmaraType MuseumPeriod Bizans
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Great Palace Mosaic Museum, just beneath the Arasta Bazaar in Sultanahmet, displays the most impressive remains of the Great Palace where Byzantine emperors lived for centuries. The mosaics once paved a large colonnaded courtyard and date to late antiquity. Hunting scenes, fighting animals, shepherds, children at play and mythical creatures are rendered with tiny pieces of stone and glass almost as vividly as paintings. Uncovered in mid-20th-century excavations and preserved in place, they are a quiet treasure between Hagia Sophia and the Blue Mosque.
