Milas İassos Balık Pazarı Müzesi

The Iasos Fish Market Museum, at ancient Iasos on Güllük bay near Milas, is a small but unusual museum inside a Roman monumental tomb – named because the building was used in recent times as a fish market. Gravestones, sculpture fragments, inscriptions and pottery from Iasos are displayed; together with the seaside ruins it shows a fishing village living within an ancient port.
