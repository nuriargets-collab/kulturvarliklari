Rumkale Örenyeri

Home › Southeastern Anatolia › Gaziantep
Location Yavuzeli, GaziantepRegion Southeastern AnatoliaType Archaeological sitePeriod Ortaçağ
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Rumkale, perched on steep cliffs where the Merzimen stream meets the Euphrates, was famed as an impregnable fortress. For a time in the Middle Ages it served as the seat of the Armenian Catholicos, before being taken by the Mamluks in the late 13th century. Church and mosque remains, a deep rock-cut well and dwellings survive inside. With the dam lake now reaching its base, it is usually visited by boat from Halfeti – an unforgettable journey on turquoise water.
