Tatlarin Yeraltı Şehri

Home › Central Anatolia › Nevşehir
Location Acıgöl, NevşehirRegion Central AnatoliaType Underground cityPeriod Bizans
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Tatlarin Underground City, in Tatlarin near Acıgöl, has wider rooms and corridors than most underground cities, suggesting it may have served as a monastery or military complex. Storerooms, kitchens, a church and spaces interpreted as toilets survive, with a church and rock buildings on the hilltop – a lesser-known, peaceful experience.
