İznik Müzesi

Home › Marmara › Bursa
Location İznik, BursaRegion MarmaraType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

İznik Museum presents the rich heritage of ancient Nicaea and later periods. Nicaea hosted two ecumenical councils; the first, in 325, was decisive in shaping one of the core texts of Christian faith. Briefly a Seljuk capital, the town became world-famous in Ottoman times for its tiles and ceramics. Roman and Byzantine sculpture, sarcophagi and inscriptions are shown alongside İznik tiles renowned for cobalt blue and coral red. A walk through the gates of the Roman walls completes the visit.
