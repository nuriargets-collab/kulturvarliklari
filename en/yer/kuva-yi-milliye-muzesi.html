Kuva-Yi Milliye Müzesi

Balıkesir Kuva-yi Milliye Museum tells the story of the popular resistance against occupation organised in western Anatolia in the first months of the War of Independence. Balıkesir became one of the key centres of the national forces through congresses held in 1919–1920, where the organisation of regional resistance was decided. Documents, photographs, weapons, clothing and personal items recall the volunteers' struggle before the regular army was formed.
