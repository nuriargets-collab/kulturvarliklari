Sümela Manastırı

Home › Black Sea › Trabzon
Location Maçka, TrabzonRegion Black SeaType Religious buildingPeriod Bizans – 20. yy
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Sümela Monastery, in Maçka amid the lush forests of the Altındere valley, clings to a sheer rock face some 300 metres above the valley floor. Tradition says two Athenian monks founded it in the late 4th century for an icon of the Virgin believed painted by St Luke. Protected and enlarged under the Empire of Trebizond and the Ottomans, it was used until 1923. Layers of frescoes covering the rock church are its treasure; reopened after long restoration, it is reached by a forest path.
