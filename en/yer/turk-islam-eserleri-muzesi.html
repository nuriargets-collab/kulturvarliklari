Türk İslam Eserleri Müzesi

Home › Marmara › Edirne
Location Merkez, EdirneRegion MarmaraType MuseumPeriod Osmanlı

Edirne Museum of Turkish and Islamic Arts occupies a medrese of the Selimiye Mosque complex, which Sinan called his masterpiece. Rooms around the arcaded courtyard display Ottoman manuscripts, calligraphy, tiles and ceramics, wood and mother-of-pearl work, weapons, costumes and jewellery. A section is devoted to the Kırkpınar oil-wrestling tournament, regarded as one of the world's oldest sporting events. Part of the UNESCO-listed Selimiye complex, the setting is as remarkable as the collection.
