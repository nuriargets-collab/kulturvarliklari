Burdur Natural History Museum focuses on a past far older than humanity: the creatures that lived here millions of years ago. The geology of the Lake District has preserved fossils from different periods, traces of extinct animals and ancient ecosystems. Fossils, rocks and minerals explain Anatolia's natural history and climate change – a different kind of time travel, especially for families and nature lovers.
