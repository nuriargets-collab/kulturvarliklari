Karakabaklı Antik Yerleşimi

Karakabaklı, in rural Silifke, is a remarkably well-preserved Roman–Byzantine village whose ancient name is unknown. Two-storey stone houses, arched doorways, a church, cisterns and tomb monuments lining the main street show the layout of an ancient village almost completely. On the road to Uzuncaburç, it is one of the most photogenic rural sites of the Olba region.
