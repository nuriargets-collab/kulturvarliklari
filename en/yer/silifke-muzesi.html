Silifke Müzesi

Home › Mediterranean › Mersin
Location Silifke, MersinRegion MediterraneanType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Silifke Museum presents the past of Silifke on the Göksu – ancient Seleucia, founded by Seleucus I and an important regional centre in Roman and Byzantine times – and of Rough Cilicia. Sculpture, gravestones, pottery and an especially rich coin collection from Olba, Diocaesarea, Korykos and other cities are displayed; a good introduction with the castle, the stone bridge and Ayatekla.
