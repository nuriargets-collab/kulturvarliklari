Edirne Museum introduces Thrace's past from prehistory to the Ottomans. A bridge between Europe and Anatolia, Thrace hosted Neolithic villages, Thracian kingdoms, Roman Hadrianopolis and Edirne, the second Ottoman capital. Pottery from mounds, Thracian objects, megalithic dolmens and menhirs, Roman sculpture and coins are displayed, with Edirne's crafts and daily life in the ethnography section. Right next to Sinan's masterpiece, the Selimiye Mosque.
