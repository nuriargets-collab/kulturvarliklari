Şanlıurfa Castle rises on a crag above Balıklıgöl in the south-west of the city. Its emblem is a pair of Roman-era Corinthian columns popularly called 'Nimrod's Throne'; legend says Abraham was hurled into the fire by a catapult set between them. A deep rock-cut moat shows the strength of its defences, and the ramparts offer superb sunset views over Balıklıgöl and old Urfa. Şanlıurfa was among the provinces affected by the 2023 earthquakes; check current status before visiting.
