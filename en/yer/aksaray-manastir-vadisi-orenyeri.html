Aksaray Manastır Vadisi Örenyeri

Location Güzelyurt, AksarayRegion Central AnatoliaType Archaeological sitePeriod Bizans
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Monastery Valley stretches beside Güzelyurt and is filled with churches, chapels and dwellings carved into the rock. Güzelyurt is associated with Gregory of Nazianzus, a leading churchman of early Christianity, and the area was a religious centre for centuries. Walking through the valley, visitors find doorways cut into rock pinnacles and slopes, some interiors still bearing traces of frescoes. Away from Cappadocia's busy routes, it rewards a slow, quiet walk.
