Ankara State Art and Sculpture Museum stands next to the Ethnography Museum in a grand building erected in the early Republic as the headquarters of the Turkish Hearths. Designed by Arif Hikmet Koyunoğlu, with tile and stone decoration and sweeping staircases, it is one of the finest examples of the First National Architectural Movement. Its collection traces Turkish painting and sculpture from the late Ottoman period to the present. The building itself is part of the experience.
