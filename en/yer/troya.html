Troya

Troy became the most famous city in world literature through Homer's Iliad. The mound of Hisarlık holds nine main layers built one upon another over some four thousand years, from the Early Bronze Age to Roman times. Heinrich Schliemann's excavations in the 1870s showed the world that the legend rested on a real place, though his hasty methods also damaged some layers. Later scientific work revealed a powerful city controlling trade between the Aegean and the Black Sea. Visitors walk among walls, ramps, megarons and a Roman theatre. UNESCO World Heritage since 1998.
