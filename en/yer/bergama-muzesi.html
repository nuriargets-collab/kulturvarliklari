Bergama Müzesi

Home › Aegean › İzmir
Location Bergama, İzmirRegion AegeanType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Bergama Museum is one of Türkiye's oldest museums and displays the rich heritage of ancient Pergamon. A highlight is the model of the Altar of Zeus, which once stood on the acropolis and is now in Berlin, helping visitors imagine its true scale and the story of its friezes. Sculpture, portraits, reliefs, coins and everyday objects reflect Pergamene and Roman art, and finds rescued from Allianoi, an ancient spa now under a dam lake, are also on display.
