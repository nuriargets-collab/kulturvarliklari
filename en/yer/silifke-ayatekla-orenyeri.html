Silifke Ayatekla Örenyeri

Home › Mediterranean › Mersin
Location Silifke, MersinRegion MediterraneanType Archaeological sitePeriod Erken Hristiyanlık / Bizans
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Ayatekla, just south of Silifke, is an important pilgrimage site regarded as where St Thecla, said to be one of the first followers of the apostle Paul, lived and died. Tradition holds she spent her last years in a cave later turned into an underground church. In the 5th century a monumental basilica was built over the holy place; its standing apse wall is visible from afar, and visitors can descend to the underground church.
