Didim Apollon Tapınağı

Home › Aegean › Aydın
Location Didim, AydınRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Temple of Apollo at Didim is the heart of Didyma, one of the ancient world's most important oracle sanctuaries, linked to Miletus by a sacred way. People came to ask Apollo about the future. The temple seen today was begun in the Hellenistic period and, despite centuries of work, never completed; its towering columns, monumental passages to the inner court and unfinished surfaces reveal how ancient builders worked. The famous Medusa head relief has become Didim's emblem.
