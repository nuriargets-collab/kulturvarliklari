Sardes Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › Manisa
Location Salihli, ManisaRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Lidya / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Sardis was the capital of the Kingdom of Lydia and city of King Croesus, legendary for his wealth. Gold dust carried by the Pactolus stream made the Lydians rich, and the world's first coins are thought to have been struck here. Western seat of Persian rule and later a Roman metropolis, Sardis greets visitors with a grand bath-gymnasium complex with a re-erected façade and one of the largest synagogues of antiquity. Outside town, the giant columns of the Temple of Artemis stand before the rocky acropolis.
