Göbeklitepe

Göbeklitepe, north-east of Şanlıurfa, is a Neolithic site about 11,500 years old that changed what we know of human history. Before farming and pottery were widespread, hunter-gatherers built monumental circular enclosures of giant T-shaped pillars carved with foxes, boars, snakes, vultures and scorpions, as well as human arms and hands – suggesting the pillars represent human figures. Inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 2018; walkways under a protective roof and a visitor centre explain the discovery. Şanlıurfa was among the provinces affected by the 2023 earthquakes; check current status before visiting.
