Zeugma Mozaik Müzesi

🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Zeugma Mosaic Museum is one of the largest mosaic museums in the world, displaying extraordinary works from ancient Zeugma on the Euphrates. Founded by Seleucus I, one of Alexander's generals, Zeugma became a wealthy Roman frontier city at a Euphrates crossing. Emergency rescue excavations as the Birecik Dam waters rose saved the mosaics decorating its villa floors. The museum's emblem, the 'Gypsy Girl' with her enigmatic gaze, is shown in a specially darkened room. Mythological scenes and wall paintings open a unique window onto Roman life and art.
