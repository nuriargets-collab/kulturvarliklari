Edirne Hıdırlık Tabya Balkan Tarihi Müzesi

Home › Marmara › Edirne
Location Merkez, EdirneRegion MarmaraType MuseumPeriod Osmanlı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Hıdırlık Bastion Museum of Balkan History occupies one of the 19th-century bastions built to defend Edirne. It played an important role in the 1877–78 Russo-Ottoman War and especially in the long siege of Edirne during the Balkan Wars, which left deep marks on the city's memory. Documents, maps and photographs tell the last two centuries of Balkan history – wars, migrations and shifting borders – while the view from the bastion takes in the city and the Meriç valley.
