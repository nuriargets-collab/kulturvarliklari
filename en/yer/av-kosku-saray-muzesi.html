The Hunting Lodge Palace Museum (Kasr-ı Hümayun) in İzmit is an elegant palace built in the second half of the 19th century as a hunting lodge for Sultan Abdülaziz. On a slope above the Gulf of İzmit, it is a fine example of Western-influenced palace architecture and period interiors, with wall and ceiling paintings and original furniture. Atatürk also stayed here on visits to İzmit. Objects relating to sultans' hunting trips and court culture are displayed.
