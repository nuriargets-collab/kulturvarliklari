Kurtuluş Savaşı Müzesi

Home › Central Anatolia › Ankara
Location Altındağ, AnkaraRegion Central AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Cumhuriyet
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The War of Independence Museum is housed in the historic building where the Grand National Assembly of Turkey first convened on 23 April 1920. This modest stone building on Ulus Square became the political centre of the national struggle: war decisions, early constitutional arrangements and the path to the Republic were shaped here. The assembly hall with its rows of wooden desks, together with documents, photographs and personal items, conveys the story of a struggle carried out against all odds.
