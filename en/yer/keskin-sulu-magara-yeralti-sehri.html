Keskin Sulu Mağara Underground City, in Keskin near Kırıkkale, is an underground settlement of rooms and passages carved into the earth. In Central Anatolia such structures, cut into soft rock, served throughout history as shelters from danger, stores for produce and refuges from harsh climate; 'Sulu' (watery) refers to a spring inside. Narrow corridors, storage niches and shared spaces show organised life below ground.
