Yeşilhisar Soğanlı Örenyeri

Home › Central Anatolia › Kayseri
Location Yeşilhisar, KayseriRegion Central AnatoliaType Archaeological sitePeriod Bizans
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Soğanlı Valley, in Yeşilhisar near Kayseri, is one of Cappadocia's lesser-known but most peaceful corners. Rock-cut churches on both slopes show intense Byzantine monastic life, some still with colourful frescoes; the Domed Church, though carved from rock, looks like a free-standing building from outside. Cliffs carved with dovecotes and colourful handmade Soğanlı dolls by local women add a warm touch. Ideal for walking and photography away from the crowds.
