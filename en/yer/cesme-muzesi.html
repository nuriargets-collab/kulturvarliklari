Çeşme Müzesi

Home › Aegean › İzmir
Location Çeşme, İzmirRegion AegeanType MuseumPeriod Osmanlı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Çeşme Museum is housed within Çeşme Castle, built in the early 16th century under Bayezid II to guard Aegean sea routes from its strategic position facing Chios. Archaeological finds from nearby ancient Erythrae are displayed alongside documents on the Battle of Çeşme of 1770, in which the Ottoman fleet suffered heavy losses against the Russian fleet. The view from the walls over the marina reveals the historic side of this holiday town.
