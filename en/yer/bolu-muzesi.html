Bolu Museum introduces the past of Bolu, site of Claudiopolis, an important city of ancient Bithynia. Antinous, the young companion of Emperor Hadrian who was deified after his death, is known to have been born here, giving Bolu special fame in antiquity. Prehistoric finds, Roman sculpture and inscriptions, coins, gravestones and ethnographic items are on display – a look at the lesser-known ancient side of a city famed for its forests and lakes.
