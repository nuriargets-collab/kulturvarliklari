Assos Örenyeri

Home › Marmara › Çanakkale
Location Ayvacık, ÇanakkaleRegion MarmaraType Archaeological sitePeriod Arkaik / Helenistik
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Assos, above the village of Behramkale, crowns a volcanic hill overlooking the Gulf of Edremit and Lesbos. The Temple of Athena on the summit is a rare example of an Archaic Doric temple in Anatolia, and watching sunset between its columns is one of the Aegean's most memorable experiences. Aristotle is known to have lived and worked here for a time. Walls descending to the harbour, agora, gymnasium, theatre and roadside sarcophagi show a complete ancient townscape; the little stone harbour below is perfect for a rest.
