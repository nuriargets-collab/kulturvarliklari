Tokat Müzesi

Home › Black Sea › Tokat
Location Merkez, TokatRegion Black SeaType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli

Tokat Museum occupies the historic Arastalı Bedesten, built in Seljuk times in the 13th century. Bronze Age and Hittite finds, Roman sculpture and gravestones, Byzantine icons and coins show the region from prehistory to the Ottomans, with Tokat's printed cloth tradition also reflected; combine with the Gök Madrasa, Taşhan and Ali Pasha Mosque.
