Ahlat Selçuklu Mezarlığı

Home › Eastern Anatolia › Bitlis
Location Ahlat, BitlisRegion Eastern AnatoliaType Archaeological sitePeriod Selçuklu / Beylikler
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Ahlat Seljuk Cemetery on Lake Van is regarded as one of the largest and most impressive cemeteries of the Turkish-Islamic world. Some of its thousands of gravestones stand well above human height, their geometric patterns, plant motifs and inscriptions showing the extraordinary skill of medieval stonemasons. The volcanic stones and the blue lake turn the site into an open-air museum, while nearby kümbets – domed or conical tomb towers – show the city's former wealth. Morning and evening light is best.
