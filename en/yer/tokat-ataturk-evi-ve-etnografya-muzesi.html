Tokat Atatürk Evi ve Etnografya Müzesi

Home › Black Sea › Tokat
Location Merkez, TokatRegion Black SeaType MuseumPeriod Osmanlı / Cumhuriyet
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Tokat Atatürk House and Ethnography Museum, in a traditional 19th-century Tokat mansion, recalls Atatürk's stay in 1919 on his way to the Sivas Congress. Besides photographs and documents, rooms display block-printing moulds and printed cloths of Tokat's famous yazma tradition, copperwork, costumes and household items under carved wooden ceilings.
