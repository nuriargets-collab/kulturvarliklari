Zeugma Örenyeri

Home › Southeastern Anatolia › Gaziantep
Location Nizip, GaziantepRegion Southeastern AnatoliaType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Zeugma, at Belkıs village near Nizip on the Euphrates, takes its name from the word for 'bridge' or 'crossing'. Founded by Seleucus I, it controlled with its twin on the opposite bank one of the Euphrates' most important crossings and in Roman times was a rich frontier city hosting a large military unit. Though dam waters cover part of it, hillside villas have been excavated and opened under a protective roof; walkways let visitors see mosaics and wall paintings preserved in situ.
