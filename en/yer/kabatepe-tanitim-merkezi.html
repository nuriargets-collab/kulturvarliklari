Kabatepe Tanıtım Merkezi

Home › Marmara › Çanakkale

TREN中文हिन्दीESعربيDE

The Kabatepe Information Centre, near Kabatepe on the Gallipoli Peninsula, presents the Gallipoli campaign through modern exhibits and simulations. The course of the war, life at the front and soldiers' stories are told audiovisually. Visiting before touring the battlefields makes the history easier to understand.
