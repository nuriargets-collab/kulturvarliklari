Hadrianopolis Örenyeri

Hadrianopolis, in the Eskipazar district of Karabük, is an important Roman and Byzantine settlement in Paphlagonia. Recent excavations have made it known especially for mosaics: floors of baths and churches decorated with animal figures, plant and geometric patterns and symbolic scenes – earning it the nickname 'Zeugma of the Black Sea region'. Its churches also shed light on early Christian life here.
