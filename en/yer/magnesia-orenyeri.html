Magnesia Örenyeri

Magnesia on the Maeander, near Germencik, is an Ionian city with important works of Hellenistic architecture. Its most famous building is the Temple of Artemis Leukophryene designed by the architect Hermogenes, whose ideas of proportion influenced later Roman architects. The Athenian statesman Themistocles governed Magnesia for the Persian king during his exile. The agora, theatre, stadium and temple precinct can be explored on foot, close to Ephesus.
