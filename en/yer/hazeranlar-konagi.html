Hazeranlar Konağı

Home › Black Sea › Amasya
Location Merkez, AmasyaRegion Black SeaType MuseumPeriod Osmanlı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Hazeranlar Mansion on the banks of the Yeşilırmak is one of the most elegant of Amasya's riverside houses. Built in the second half of the 19th century, it is named after Hazeran Hanım, who lived here. Carved wooden ceilings, bay-windowed rooms and the separation of family and guest quarters reflect Ottoman mansion life in detail. Now an ethnographic museum, its furnished rooms recreate daily life, while the windows frame the river and the royal tombs across the valley.
