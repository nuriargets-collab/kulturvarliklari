Mudanya Mütareke Evi Müzesi

Home › Marmara › Bursa
Location Mudanya, BursaRegion MarmaraType MuseumPeriod Cumhuriyet
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Mudanya Armistice House Museum is the historic building where the Armistice of Mudanya, ending the military phase of the War of Independence, was signed in autumn 1922. In this elegant seaside mansion, Turkish and Allied representatives negotiated for days; the agreement opened the way to recovering Eastern Thrace without war and to the Lausanne Peace Conference. The negotiation room, period furniture, photographs and documents are on display.
