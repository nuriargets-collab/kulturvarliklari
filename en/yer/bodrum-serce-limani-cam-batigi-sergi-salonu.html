Bodrum Serçe Limanı Cam Batığı Sergi Salonu

Location Bodrum, MuğlaRegion AegeanType MuseumPeriod Ortaçağ (11. yy)
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Serçe Limanı Glass Wreck Hall, in Bodrum Castle, displays an 11th-century merchant ship that sank at Serçe Limanı near Marmaris, with its wooden hull carefully raised, conserved and reassembled. The ship carried large amounts of raw and broken glass – hence the name – and reconstructed glass vessels and crew belongings offer rare insight into medieval eastern Mediterranean trade and shipbuilding.
