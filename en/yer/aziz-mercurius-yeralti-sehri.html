Aziz Mercurius Yeraltı Şehri

Saint Mercurius Underground City lies near Güzelyurt, at the western edge of Cappadocia, where Greek and Turkish families lived side by side for centuries. Like other underground settlements here, it consists of rooms, narrow passages, ventilation shafts and storage areas carved level by level into soft volcanic rock, used as shelters in times of danger. Less crowded than Derinkuyu or Kaymaklı, it lets visitors feel the quiet, mysterious atmosphere of underground life. Passages are narrow and low.
