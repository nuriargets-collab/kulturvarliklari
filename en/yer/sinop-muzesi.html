Sinop Museum presents the seafaring history of Sinop, on the peninsula at Anatolia's northernmost tip. Ancient Sinope, founded by Milesian colonists in the 7th century BC, was a key Black Sea port and home of the philosopher Diogenes. Hellenistic and Roman sculpture, gravestones, amphorae, coins, mosaics and Byzantine icons are displayed; the garden holds remains of a Temple of Serapis.
