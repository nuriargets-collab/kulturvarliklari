Giresun Müzesi

Home › Black Sea › Giresun
Location Merkez, GiresunRegion Black SeaType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Giresun Museum is housed in the restored 19th-century former Gogora Church in a historic seaside quarter; its high ceilings and arched windows give the museum a special atmosphere. Archaeological works, coins and ethnographic items trace the eastern Black Sea's past from antiquity to the Ottomans. Combined with Giresun Castle and a seaside walk, it reveals the historic side of a city famed for hazelnuts; Giresun Island offshore was linked in antiquity with Amazon legends.
