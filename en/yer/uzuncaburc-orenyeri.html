Uzuncaburç Örenyeri

Home › Mediterranean › Mersin
Location Silifke, MersinRegion MediterraneanType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Uzuncaburç, north of Silifke on cool Taurus plateaus, holds the remains of ancient Olba-Diocaesarea, a Hellenistic sacred centre ruled by the priest-kings of Olba. Its Temple of Zeus Olbios is regarded as one of the earliest Corinthian temples in Anatolia, with most columns still standing, and a five-storey Hellenistic tower – the 'tall tower' that names the village – is its emblem. A monumental gate, colonnaded street, theatre and Temple of Tyche rise among village houses.
