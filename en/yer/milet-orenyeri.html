Milet Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › Aydın
Location Didim, AydınRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Arkaik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Miletus was one of antiquity's most influential cities, founding dozens of colonies from the Black Sea to Egypt, and is remembered as the birthplace of philosophy: Thales, Anaximander and Anaximenes lived here, as did Hippodamus, father of urban planning. Once a city of four harbours, it was left inland by silt from the Büyük Menderes. Its great theatre, the Baths of Faustina, the agora and the elegant 15th-century İlyas Bey Mosque reveal its many layers.
