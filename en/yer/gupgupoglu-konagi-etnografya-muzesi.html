Güpgüpoğlu Konağı Etnografya Müzesi

Home › Central Anatolia › Kayseri
Location Melikgazi, KayseriRegion Central AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Osmanlı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Güpgüpoğlu Mansion Ethnography Museum occupies one of the oldest and most impressive surviving mansions in Kayseri. Built of dark volcanic stone, it reflects the city's distinctive stone domestic architecture and the life of wealthy families; its room layout and carved woodwork show fine craftsmanship. Costumes, jewellery, carpets, kitchenware and everyday scenes are on display – a window onto home life amid Kayseri's Seljuk monuments.
