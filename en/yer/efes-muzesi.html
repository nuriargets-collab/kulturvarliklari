Ephesus Museum in central Selçuk, displaying finds from Ephesus and its surroundings, is one of Türkiye's most visited museums. Its most famous works are the statues of Artemis of Ephesus, known for the many egg- or fruit-shaped symbols on her chest – the city's distinctive image of the goddess of fertility and protection. Everyday objects, portraits, coins and small finds from the Terrace Houses feel like a visit to wealthy Ephesian homes, and a section on the city's gladiator cemetery reveals the reality of life in the arena.
