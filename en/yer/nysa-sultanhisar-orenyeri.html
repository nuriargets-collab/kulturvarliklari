Nysa Sultanhisar Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › Aydın
Location Sultanhisar, AydınRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Nysa, north of Sultanhisar among olive groves, was built on both sides of a stream valley, its halves linked by a bridge and a long tunnel carrying the stream beneath the city – a unique feat of engineering. An important centre of learning, it is where the geographer Strabo studied. One of Anatolia's best-preserved ancient library buildings, a theatre with a carved stage building, the council house and gymnasium make for a peaceful walk. The Dionysus friezes of the theatre stand out.
