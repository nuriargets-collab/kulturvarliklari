Alaşehir Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › Manisa
Location Alaşehir, ManisaRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Bizans
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Alaşehir preserves remains of ancient Philadelphia, founded by King Attalus II of Pergamon. One of the seven churches of Revelation, it is best represented by the massive brick piers of a great Byzantine basilica rising in the town centre. Alaşehir held out as one of Byzantium's last strongholds in Anatolia until the late Middle Ages before becoming Ottoman. Amid vineyards, its piers rising between modern houses show history woven into daily life.
