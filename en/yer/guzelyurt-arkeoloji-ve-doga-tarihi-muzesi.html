Güzelyurt Archaeology and Natural History Museum occupies the former bishop's residence right beside the Church of St Mamas in Güzelyurt (Morphou). The ground floor holds a natural history collection of the region's birds and other animals, the upper floor archaeological finds from the Bronze Age to Roman times; a statue of Artemis found at Salamis is among the highlights. The adjoining Church of St Mamas, blending Byzantine and Gothic elements, can be visited as an icon museum with the saint's sarcophagus. Güzelyurt, famed for its citrus groves, is a good stop on the way to Soli and Vouni.
