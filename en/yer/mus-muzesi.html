Muş Museum presents thousands of years of history of the fertile Muş plain in eastern Anatolia, from Urartian settlements through Armenian, Byzantine, Seljuk and Ottoman times. Prehistoric tools, Urartian pottery and metalwork, medieval finds, coins and ethnography – carpets, kilims, costumes – are displayed.
