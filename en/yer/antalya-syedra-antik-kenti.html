Antalya Syedra Antik Kenti

Home › Mediterranean › Antalya
Location Alanya, AntalyaRegion MediterraneanType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma

Syedra is an ancient Cilician city on a hill overlooking the sea east of Alanya. Its position allowed control of both sea trade and the coastal road, making it one of the region's important settlements. Visitors walk among a colonnaded street, large cisterns, baths and houses with views over the coastline and banana groves below. Ongoing excavations have revealed colourful wall paintings and rock-cut spaces. A quiet, scenic site a short drive from Alanya's beaches.
