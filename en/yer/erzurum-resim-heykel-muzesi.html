Erzurum State Art and Sculpture Museum brings the development of Turkish painting and sculpture to visitors in this long-established cultural city of eastern Anatolia. Works by artists of different generations trace Turkish art from the late Ottoman period to the present, while temporary exhibitions contribute to the city's cultural life – a different artistic experience amid Erzurum's Seljuk and Ottoman monuments.
