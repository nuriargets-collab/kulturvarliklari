Niğde Müzesi

Home › Central Anatolia › Niğde
Location Merkez, NiğdeRegion Central AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Niğde Museum presents Niğde, the southern gateway to Cappadocia. A notable section shows naturally mummified bodies found in Cappadocian churches, including one thought to be a Byzantine-era nun. Prehistoric finds from mounds such as Köşk Höyük, Hittite and Phrygian objects, Roman and Byzantine sculpture and coins complete the collection.
