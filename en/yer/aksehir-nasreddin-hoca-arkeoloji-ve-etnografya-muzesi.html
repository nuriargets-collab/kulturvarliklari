Akşehir Nasreddin Hoca Arkeoloji and Ethnography Museum

The Akşehir Nasreddin Hodja Archaeology and Ethnography Museum presents the long history of Akşehir, where the world-famous humorist Nasreddin Hodja lived and is buried. Known in antiquity as Philomelion, the town became an important Seljuk caravan stop, as its Stone Medrese and other buildings show. Archaeological finds are displayed with local costumes, crafts and everyday objects; the annual Nasreddin Hodja Festival in July adds colour.
