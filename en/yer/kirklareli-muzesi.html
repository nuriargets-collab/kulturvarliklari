Kırklareli Museum holds one of Türkiye's most important collections on Thrace's prehistory. Long excavations at sites such as Aşağı Pınar and Kanlıgeçit have provided key evidence on the spread of farming into Europe and Early Bronze Age town life. Pottery, tools and house models from these digs are shown with Thracian works and information on the region's dolmens, while the ethnography section presents Thrace's colourful costumes and traditions.
