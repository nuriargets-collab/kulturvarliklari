Kahta Yenikale Örenyeri

Home › Southeastern Anatolia › Adıyaman
Location Kahta, AdıyamanRegion Southeastern AnatoliaType Archaeological sitePeriod Kommagene / Ortaçağ
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Yeni Kale is an impressive fortress perched on a steep rock above the deep valley of the Kahta River. The site's strategic value was known since antiquity and it is thought to have been used in Commagenian times, but its present appearance largely comes from medieval building and repairs, notably under the Mamluks. Rock-cut stairs, cisterns and a hidden passage down to the valley show how it withstood sieges. The climb takes effort, but the view from the top is well worth it.
