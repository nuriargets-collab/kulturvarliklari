Safranbolu City History Museum occupies the old government building in the castle area of UNESCO-listed Safranbolu. Enriched in Ottoman times by its position on a caravan route, Safranbolu preserves hundreds of timber and mud-brick mansions, bazaars and inns – one of the best-kept Ottoman townscapes. Recreated shops, objects and documents tell of its crafts, trade and mansion culture, and the view over red-tiled roofs explains why the town is so special.
