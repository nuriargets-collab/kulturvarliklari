Kocaeli Archaeological Museum presents the glorious past of Nicomedia, ancient İzmit. Founded in the 3rd century BC as capital of the Kingdom of Bithynia, it became the eastern capital of the empire under Diocletian's tetrarchy. Among its treasures are relief friezes found during construction in the city, depicting imperial ceremonies with traces of vivid paint – rare examples of Roman art. Sculpture, sarcophagi, mosaics and coins reveal the splendour of an imperial capital now beneath an industrial city.
