Silifke Narlıkuyu Mozaik Müzesi

Home › Mediterranean › Mersin
Location Silifke, MersinRegion MediterraneanType MuseumPeriod Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Narlıkuyu Mosaic Museum, in the cove of Narlıkuyu near Silifke, protects in place the mosaic floor of a 4th-century Roman bath. Its most famous scene shows the Three Graces, symbols of beauty and joy in mythology, after whom the mosaic is named; inscriptions record who built the bath and the virtues of its spring water. Ideal for a short stop with the nearby Heaven and Hell sinkholes.
