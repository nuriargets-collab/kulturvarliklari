Cehennemağzı Mağaraları

Home › Black Sea › Zonguldak

TREN中文हिन्दीESعربيDE

The Cehennemağzı ('Mouth of Hell') Caves, at Karadeniz Ereğli near ancient Heraclea Pontica, are three caves where, according to myth, Heracles dragged up Cerberus, the three-headed guardian of the underworld, as one of his twelve labours. One cave was turned into a Byzantine church with fresco traces – myth and nature intertwined.
