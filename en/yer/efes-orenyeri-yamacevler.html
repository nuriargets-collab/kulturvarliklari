The Terrace Houses of Ephesus, beside Curetes Street, are luxurious homes of wealthy Roman families built in terraces up the slope. Under a modern protective roof, colourful frescoes, mosaic floors and marble cladding reflect the opulent domestic life of the ancient world with remarkable vividness. Glass walkways let visitors look down into courtyards, dining rooms and private baths, and in one house the long-term reassembly of thousands of marble fragments, like a jigsaw, can be watched. Though it costs extra, it is one of the most impressive parts of Ephesus.
