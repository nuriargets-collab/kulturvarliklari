Ihlara Vadisi Örenyeri

Home › Central Anatolia › Aksaray
Location Güzelyurt, AksarayRegion Central AnatoliaType Archaeological sitePeriod Bizans
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Ihlara Valley is a deep canyon carved by the Melendiz stream through volcanic tuff from Mount Hasan. Dozens of rock-cut churches in its steep walls reveal an intense monastic life in Byzantine times; frescoes in churches such as Ağaçaltı, Sümbüllü, Yılanlı and Kokar still tell biblical stories. Visitors descend by stairs and walk along the stream under poplars and willows, ending with a rest in Belisırma village – a peaceful alternative to Cappadocia's busier spots.
