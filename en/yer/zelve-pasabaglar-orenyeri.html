Zelve - Paşabağlar Örenyeri

Home › Central Anatolia › Nevşehir
Location Avanos, NevşehirRegion Central AnatoliaType Archaeological sitePeriod Bizans / OsmanlıUNESCO 1985
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Zelve and Paşabağları offer Cappadocia's most striking fairy-chimney scenery. Zelve is a rock village across three valleys, inhabited until the mid-20th century when erosion forced residents to move; abandoned rock houses, churches, a rock-cut mosque and a mill show everyday life. Paşabağları is famed for multi-capped, mushroom-shaped chimneys, one with a chapel where a hermit is said to have lived. Part of the UNESCO-listed Göreme National Park.
