Erzincan Museum introduces the past of this fertile plain at the headwaters of the Euphrates. Its key pieces are Urartian finds from nearby Altıntepe, known for its temple, palace and rich tombs as one of the most important western centres of the Urartian kingdom. Bronze shields, belts, horse harness and ornaments show the Urartians' famous metalworking skill. Medieval and Ottoman works and local ethnography are also on display.
