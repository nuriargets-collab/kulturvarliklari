Tire Müzesi

Home › Aegean › İzmir
Location Tire, İzmirRegion AegeanType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Tire Museum presents the past of Tire, on the fertile Küçük Menderes plain, an important cultural and trading centre under the Aydınids and early Ottomans. Tire is known for its historic mosques, inns and baths and its Tuesday market, one of the most colourful in the Aegean. Archaeological finds from nearby ancient cities are shown with ethnographic items reflecting traditional crafts such as felt-making and weaving.
