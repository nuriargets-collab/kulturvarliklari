Anamur Museum is a small museum on the past of Anamur at the southernmost point of the Mediterranean coast. Much of its collection comes from nearby Anemurium: pottery, glass, coins, jewellery and grave goods reflect daily life in Roman and Byzantine times, while the ethnography section shows costumes, crafts and household items of people living between the Taurus and the sea.
