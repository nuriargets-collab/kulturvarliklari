Anemurium Örenyeri

Home › Mediterranean › Mersin
Location Anamur, MersinRegion MediterraneanType Archaeological sitePeriod Roma / Bizans
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Anemurium, at Cape Anamur – the southernmost tip of Anatolia – lies between sea and mountains. Its most striking feature is a vast necropolis of hundreds of tomb buildings along the slope, some still with painted walls and traces of mosaic. Baths, an odeon, a theatre, aqueducts and church remains show lively Roman and early Byzantine life. Next to a beach where sea turtles nest, its tomb houses facing the sea are unforgettable in late-afternoon light.
