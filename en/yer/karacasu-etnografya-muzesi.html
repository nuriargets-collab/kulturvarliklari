Karacasu Etnografya Müzesi

Home › Aegean › Aydın
Location Karacasu, AydınRegion AegeanType MuseumPeriod Yakın Dönem
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Karacasu Ethnography Museum presents the local culture of Karacasu, on the road to Aphrodisias. The town is especially known for its pottery tradition, passed down through generations and closely tied to the land and way of life. Costumes, household goods, farming tools and crafts recreate recent daily life – a human-scale story after the great marble monuments of antiquity.
