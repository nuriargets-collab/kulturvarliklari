Söğüt Ertuğrul Gazi Müzesi

Home › Marmara › Bilecik
Location Söğüt, BilecikRegion MarmaraType MuseumPeriod Osmanlı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Söğüt Ertuğrul Gazi Museum, in Söğüt – regarded as the first seat of the Ottoman principality – introduces the founding years of the state. Söğüt was the homeland of Ertuğrul Gazi, father of Osman Bey, whose tomb is here; annual commemorations keep this link alive. Documents, coins, weapons, clothing and ethnographic items tell of the first steps of a small frontier principality that would become an empire spanning three continents.
