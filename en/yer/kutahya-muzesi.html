Kütahya Müzesi

Kütahya Museum is housed in the Vacidiye Medrese, built in the early 14th century under the Germiyanids and known as a school where astronomy was taught. Its domed central hall and surrounding rooms hold works from thousands of years of regional history. The most famous is a magnificent Roman sarcophagus from Aizanoi showing the legendary battle between Amazons and Greeks in deep relief. Prehistoric finds, Phrygian and Roman works, coins and jewellery are also displayed.
