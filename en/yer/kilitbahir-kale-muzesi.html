Kilitbahir Kale Müzesi

Home › Marmara › Çanakkale
Location Eceabat, ÇanakkaleRegion MarmaraType MuseumPeriod Osmanlı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Kilitbahir Castle, on the European shore at the narrowest point of the Dardanelles, was built in the 15th century by Sultan Mehmed the Conqueror. Its name means 'lock of the sea'; with Çimenlik Castle opposite, it was designed to control passage through the strait. Its inner keep has a unique plan resembling a clover or heart from above. Restored and open as a museum, it lets visitors watch ships passing from its walls; a short ferry ride from Çanakkale.
