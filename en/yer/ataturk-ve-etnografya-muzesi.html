Atatürk ve Etnografya Müzesi

Home › Aegean › Uşak
Location Merkez, UşakRegion AegeanType MuseumPeriod Cumhuriyet
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Uşak Atatürk and Ethnography Museum occupies a historic house where Atatürk stayed in 1922 after the Great Offensive; it was near Uşak that the commander-in-chief of the Greek army was captured. Photographs and documents are shown alongside world-famous Uşak carpets, local costumes and household items.
