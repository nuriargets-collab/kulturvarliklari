İstanbul Galata Kulesi

Home › Marmara › İstanbul
Location Beyoğlu, İstanbulRegion MarmaraType Castle & historic buildingPeriod Ceneviz / Osmanlı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Galata Tower is one of the most familiar symbols of Istanbul's skyline, built in the 14th century by the Genoese of Galata as the highest point of their colony's walls. Used for centuries as a watch and fire tower, it was repaired many times after fires and earthquakes and received its conical cap. The tale of Hezarfen Ahmed Çelebi flying from here to Üsküdar with artificial wings in the 17th century made it legendary. Now a museum with exhibitions on the history of Istanbul and Galata, its real reward is the 360-degree view over the Golden Horn, the historic peninsula and the Bosphorus.
