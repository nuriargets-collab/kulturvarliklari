Konya Arkeoloji Müzesi

Home › Central Anatolia › Konya
Location Meram, KonyaRegion Central AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Konya Archaeological Museum presents thousands of years of history of Konya and its surroundings on one of Anatolia's widest plains. Finds from Çatalhöyük, one of humanity's earliest town-like settlements, Bronze Age works and Roman statues, sarcophagi and mosaics are displayed. A highlight is a Roman sarcophagus depicting the twelve labours of Heracles in fine relief. Coins, jewellery and inscriptions illuminate life when Konya was called Iconium.
