Nilüfer Hatun İmareti Türk İslam Eserleri Müzesi

The Nilüfer Hatun İmaret in İznik is a soup kitchen and guesthouse built in the late 14th century by Murad I in the name of his mother Nilüfer Hatun. One of the finest examples of early Ottoman architecture, it stands out for its arcaded porch, domed spaces and alternating brick-and-stone walls. Nilüfer Hatun is known as one of the first powerful women named in Ottoman history. Now a museum of Turkish and Islamic arts, it displays examples of İznik's famous tiles and ceramics.
