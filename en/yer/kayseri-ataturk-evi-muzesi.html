Kayseri Atatürk House Museum

Kayseri Atatürk Evi Müzesi

Kayseri Atatürk House Museum is set in a historic Kayseri house where Atatürk stayed during his visit to the city. Built of cut stone, it reflects the solid, simple character of local domestic architecture. Photographs, documents and period-furnished rooms recall his days in Kayseri – a brief, meaningful stop in the busy centre.
