Xanthos Archaeological Site

Xanthos was Lycia's most important city and long its administrative centre. Set on a slope above the Eşen valley, it is remembered for its freedom-loving people: ancient sources say the Xanthians twice burned their city rather than surrender. Visitors see uniquely Lycian pillar tombs, including the Harpy Monument and the inscribed pillar bearing the longest known Lycian text. Some monuments, such as the Nereid Monument, were taken abroad in the 19th century. UNESCO World Heritage with Letoon since 1988.

🎧 A day in Xanthos

The year is 380 BC. You walk up from the Eşen valley towards the city. On the slope, tombs set on top of tall stone pillars rise towards the sky.

You stop before the inscribed pillar beside the agora. All four faces are covered with lines in Lycian, telling of the city's wars and leaders.

In the afternoon you go to see the new Nereid Monument: a small temple on a high podium, with statues of sea nymphs in wind-blown robes between its columns.

Today the inscribed pillar still stands in place. The Nereid Monument, however, was taken apart in the 19th century and shipped to London; only its foundation remains on site.

🚌 Getting there

Xanthos is next to the village of Kınık in the Kaş district of Antalya, on the Fethiye–Kaş road about 45 km from Fethiye. Get off a Fethiye–Kaş bus at Kınık and walk a short way.

⭐ Don't miss

  • The Harpy Tomb and pillar tombs
  • The Roman theatre
  • The Lycian acropolis
  • The inscribed Xanthian Obelisk (the longest Lycian text)
  • The foundations of the Nereid Monument

🏺 Finds and artworks

  • The façade of the Nereid Monument, the original Harpy Tomb reliefs and other major sculptures were taken by Charles Fellows in the 19th century and are now in the British Museum in London
  • The Harpy reliefs on site are casts

👤 Notable people

  • Capital of Lycia
  • According to Herodotus its people chose mass suicide rather than surrender to the Persian commander Harpagus in 545 BC
  • A UNESCO World Heritage Site together with Letoon

💡 Tips

The site has no shade; go in the morning or late afternoon. Visit nearby Letoon the same day; Patara is 15 minutes away.
