Fethiye Müzesi

Home › Marmara › İstanbul
Location Fatih, İstanbulRegion MarmaraType MuseumPeriod Bizans
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Fethiye Museum, in Istanbul's Çarşamba district, occupies the side chapel (parekklesion) of the building known in Byzantine times as the church of the Pammakaristos Monastery. Its gold-ground mosaics from the early 14th century are among the finest examples of Palaiologan art, the last brilliant phase of Byzantine art: Christ and the prophets in the dome, saints and Gospel scenes on the walls. The main building became a mosque, Fethiye Mosque, in the Ottoman period. A must for lovers of Byzantine art.
