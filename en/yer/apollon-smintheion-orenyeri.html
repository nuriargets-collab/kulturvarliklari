Apollon Smintheion Örenyeri

Home › Marmara › Çanakkale
Location Ayvacık, ÇanakkaleRegion MarmaraType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Apollo Smintheion, in the village of Gülpınar, is a sanctuary of Apollo 'Smintheus', associated with mice and plague. In Homer's Iliad, Apollo's priest Chryses calls upon this god, suggesting the sanctuary's ancient origins. The Ionic temple seen today is Hellenistic, and careful excavation and restoration have re-erected some of its columns. A small site museum shows frieze fragments depicting scenes from the Iliad. A must on the way to Cape Baba facing Lesbos.
