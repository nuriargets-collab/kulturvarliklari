Elmalı Müzesi

Home › Mediterranean › Antalya
Location Elmalı, AntalyaRegion MediterraneanType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Elmalı Museum tells the story of the Elmalı plateau in the Taurus Mountains, known for apple orchards and a cool climate. Settled for thousands of years in inland Lycia, the area has yielded finds from Bronze Age villages to painted tomb chambers of antiquity, and is remembered for a famous coin hoard smuggled abroad and returned after a long legal battle. Together with the town's historic mosques and houses, it reveals a different face of Antalya.
