Harran Örenyeri

Harran, south of Şanlıurfa, is considered one of the world's oldest cities, with thousands of years of continuous settlement. A cult centre of the moon god Sin in ancient Mesopotamia, it is named in scripture among the places where Abraham lived. In the Middle Ages it became a centre of astronomy, mathematics and medicine, recalled by the Great Mosque and madrasa remains. Most beloved are the domed mud-brick 'beehive' houses suited to the hot climate; the castle and walls can also be seen. Şanlıurfa was among the provinces affected by the 2023 earthquakes; check current status before visiting.
