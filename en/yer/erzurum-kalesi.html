Erzurum Castle, at the city's highest point, was built to defend the Byzantine town of Theodosiopolis and later repaired and used by the Saltukids, Seljuks and Ottomans. A tower inside, which served as both minaret and clock tower, is one of Erzurum's emblems. From the walls one sees the Double Minaret Medrese, the Great Mosque, the Three Tombs and the wide plain at a glance – strong testimony to Erzurum's strategic role on the Silk Road and the eastern frontier.
