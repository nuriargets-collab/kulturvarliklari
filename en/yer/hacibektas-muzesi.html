Hacıbektaş Müzesi

Home › Central Anatolia › Nevşehir
Location Hacıbektaş, NevşehirRegion Central AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Selçuklu / Osmanlı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Hacıbektaş Museum occupies the tomb and lodge complex of the 13th-century mystic Hacı Bektaş Veli, a central figure of the Alevi-Bektashi tradition known for teachings of love, tolerance and humanity. The tomb, retreat cell, assembly house, kitchen and courtyards display lodge objects, manuscripts, instruments and garments; annual commemorations each August draw thousands.
