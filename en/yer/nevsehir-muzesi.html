Nevşehir Müzesi

Home › Central Anatolia › Nevşehir
Location Merkez, NevşehirRegion Central AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Nevşehir Museum is a modern museum presenting Cappadocia from prehistory to the Ottomans. The volcanic landscape allowed people to carve dwellings into rock for millennia. Neolithic and Bronze Age finds, Hittite and Phrygian objects, Roman and Byzantine sculpture, coins and jewellery and local ethnography offer an overview before visiting fairy chimneys and rock churches.
