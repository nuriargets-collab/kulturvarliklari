Aigai Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › Manisa
Location Yunusemre, ManisaRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Aigai, in the Yuntdağı area of Manisa, is an Aeolian city on a forested mountain ridge that flourished under the Kingdom of Pergamon. Its most impressive structure is a multi-storey market building set against the slope, with shops and stores stacked one above the other – a fine piece of Hellenistic engineering. A theatre, council house, temples and walls can also be seen, far from the crowds amid mountain scenery.
