Amintas Kaya Mezarı

The Rock Tomb of Amyntas, carved into the cliffs above central Fethiye, is one of Lycia's most impressive rock tombs. Belonging to ancient Telmessos and dated to the 4th century BC, it imitates a temple front with two Ionic columns and a pediment; an inscription names Amyntas. Reached by steps, it offers a panorama of Fethiye bay, especially at sunset when the stone turns golden.
