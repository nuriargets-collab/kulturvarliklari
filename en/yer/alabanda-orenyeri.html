Alabanda Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › Aydın
Location Çine, AydınRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Alabanda is a Carian city spread through and around today's village of Doğanyurt in the Çine valley, where ruins and village houses intertwine. Its most striking building is the council house with high surviving walls – tangible evidence of civic decision-making in Carian cities. A theatre, temple remains and city walls can also be seen. A chance to explore an ancient city through the life of a village, far from the crowds.
