Perge Örenyeri

Home › Mediterranean › Antalya
Location Aksu, AntalyaRegion MediterraneanType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Perge was one of the grandest cities of the Pamphylian plain and is easy to visit from Antalya. You enter through a monumental gate flanked by two round Hellenistic towers, then walk a long colonnaded street with a water channel running down its centre. The stadium is one of the best preserved in Anatolia, and the theatre, baths and agora complete the picture. Statues from Perge are among the treasures of Antalya Museum, and the apostle Paul visited the city on his travels.
