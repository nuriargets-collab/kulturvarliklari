Gümüşler Manastırı ve Yeraltı Şehri

Home › Central Anatolia › Niğde
Location Merkez, NiğdeRegion Central AnatoliaType Underground cityPeriod Bizans
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Gümüşler Monastery, in Gümüşler near Niğde, is a rock-cut complex considered one of the best-preserved monasteries of the Cappadocia region. Living rooms, kitchen, stores and a church around a courtyard show monastic life as a whole; the church frescoes include a Virgin and Child sometimes called the 'smiling Mary'. Underground passages below can also be visited.
