Karain Mağarası

Home › Mediterranean › Antalya
Location Döşemealtı, AntalyaRegion MediterraneanType Natural sitePeriod Paleolitik
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Karain Cave, north of Antalya at the foot of Katran Mountain, is considered one of Türkiye's most important prehistoric sites. It shows continuous human presence reaching back hundreds of thousands of years: stone tools, hearths, animal bones and Neanderthal remains shed light on Anatolia's earliest people. Excavations began in the 1940s. A small museum near the entrance displays finds; a short, steep path leads up, rewarded by the cool interior and views of the plain.
