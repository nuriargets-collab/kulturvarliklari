Şanlıurfa Arkeoloji Müzesi

Şanlıurfa Archaeology Museum is one of Türkiye's largest museum complexes and of world importance for its Neolithic collection. Finds from Göbeklitepe, Nevalı Çori, Karahantepe and other Stone Hills sites include the 'Balıklıgöl Statue', about 11,000 years old and among the oldest known life-size human statues. A full-scale replica of a Göbeklitepe enclosure shows how the site looked, and Assyrian, Babylonian, Hittite, Roman, Byzantine and Islamic works tell Mesopotamia's continuous story. Şanlıurfa was among the provinces affected by the 2023 earthquakes; check current status before visiting.
