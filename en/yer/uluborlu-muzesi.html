Uluborlu Museum is a small museum presenting the past of Uluborlu, one of Isparta's historic towns, and its surroundings. Known in antiquity also as Apollonia, the town – with its hilltop castle and traditional houses in narrow streets – is one of the Lake District's most characteristic. Archaeological finds, coins and ethnographic items tell of life in different periods.
