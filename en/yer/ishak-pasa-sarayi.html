İshak Paşa Sarayı

Home › Eastern Anatolia › Ağrı
Location Doğubayazıt, AğrıRegion Eastern AnatoliaType Castle & historic buildingPeriod Osmanlı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

İshak Pasha Palace stands on a hillside above Doğubayazıt, facing the majestic silhouette of Mount Ararat, and is one of Anatolia's most fairy-tale-like buildings. Completed by the regional governor İshak Pasha, it reached its present form in the late 18th century. It combines Ottoman, Seljuk, Persian and Caucasian traditions: richly carved portals, a harem, mosque, bath, kitchens and the family tomb sit side by side. It is said to have been designed with heating and drainage systems remarkable for its time. At sunset the stone walls glow golden.
