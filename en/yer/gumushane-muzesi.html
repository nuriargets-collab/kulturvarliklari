Gümüşhane Museum presents the history of this mountain city, named after the silver mines worked here for centuries. Gümüşhane lies on the historic caravan route linking Trabzon with Iran and inner Anatolia, and mining and trade shaped its identity. Archaeological finds, coins and traces of the mining tradition are shown with local costumes, crafts and everyday objects – a good start for discovering the town's lesser-known heritage.
