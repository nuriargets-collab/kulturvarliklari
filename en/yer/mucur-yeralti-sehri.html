Mucur Yeraltı Şehri

Mucur Underground City, in Mucur near Kırşehir, consists of rooms and connecting passages carved into soft volcanic tuff. At the northern edge of the Cappadocia region, it exemplifies the centuries-old local tradition of underground shelter and storage. Ventilation shafts, granaries and shared spaces show organised life underground – a quiet alternative to the famous sites.
