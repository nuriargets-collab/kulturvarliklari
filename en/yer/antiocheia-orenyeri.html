Antiocheia Örenyeri

Location Yalvaç, IspartaRegion MediterraneanType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma

Pisidian Antioch, beside Yalvaç at the foot of the Sultan Mountains, was one of the region's most important Roman cities. Emperor Augustus made it a colony for veteran soldiers, giving it the look of a 'little Rome'. The semicircular precinct of the Temple of Augustus, a monumental gate, theatre, baths and a long aqueduct survive. It also matters in Christian history: the apostle Paul is said to have preached here on his first journey. Fragments of the famous inscription recording Augustus's deeds were found here.
