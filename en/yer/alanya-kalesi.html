Alanya Kalesi

🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Alanya Castle is a vast defensive system on a rocky peninsula dominating the city's skyline. Used since antiquity, it was taken in 1221 by Seljuk Sultan Alaeddin Keykubad I and turned into a winter capital and port called Alaiye. Kilometres of walls with hundreds of towers, the inner castle and palace ruins show Seljuk naval power, while the octagonal Red Tower and the rock-cut Seljuk shipyard are among Anatolia's most remarkable medieval coastal structures. Sunset over the Mediterranean from the castle is unforgettable.
