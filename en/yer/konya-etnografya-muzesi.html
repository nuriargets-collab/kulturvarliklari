Konya Etnografya Müzesi

Home › Central Anatolia › Konya
Location Karatay, KonyaRegion Central AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Osmanlı / Yakın Dönem
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Konya Ethnography Museum presents recent everyday culture in the former Seljuk capital and its surroundings. Konya was for centuries a centre of crafts – carpets and kilims, copperwork, wood carving and manuscript arts. Costumes, jewellery, carpets, weapons, household items, calligraphy and handicrafts are displayed – a complement to the great monuments, focusing on ordinary people and craft traditions.
