Taxiarchis Monument Museum is a grand 19th-century Orthodox church rising among the narrow stone-house streets of Ayvalık. Named after the archangels, it reflects the community life and architecture of the era when Ayvalık grew rich on olive-oil trade. High ceilings, stonework and interior elements revealed by restoration make it one of the most impressive witnesses to the town's multicultural past. The climb up through colourful doors and old houses is part of the pleasure.
