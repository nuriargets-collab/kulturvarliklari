Kayseri Arkeoloji Müzesi

Home › Central Anatolia › Kayseri
Location Kocasinan, KayseriRegion Central AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Kayseri Archaeological Museum presents the rich past of this ancient city at the foot of Mount Erciyes. Its treasures include tablets from the Assyrian trading colony at nearby Kültepe – Anatolia's oldest known written documents, recording merchants' letters, contracts and accounts from some four thousand years ago. Another star is a magnificent Roman sarcophagus depicting the twelve labours of Heracles. Hittite, Phrygian, Roman and Byzantine sculpture, pottery and jewellery complete the collection.
