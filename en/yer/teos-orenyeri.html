Teos Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › İzmir
Location Seferihisar, İzmirRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Arkaik / Helenistik
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Teos, near Sığacık in Seferihisar, was an important Ionian city on a peninsula with two harbours. Devoted to Dionysus, god of wine and theatre, it has one of the largest temples of Dionysus in the ancient world, designed by the famous Hermogenes. Teos was also home to a renowned guild of Dionysian artists – actors and musicians – and birthplace of the lyric poet Anacreon. The temple, theatre, odeon and council house among olive groves, together with Sığacık's walled village, make for a delightful day.
