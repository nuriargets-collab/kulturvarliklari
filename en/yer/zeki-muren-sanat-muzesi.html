Zeki Müren Sanat Müzesi

Home › Aegean › Muğla
Location Bodrum, MuğlaRegion AegeanType MuseumPeriod Cumhuriyet
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Zeki Müren Art Museum is set in the Bodrum house where Zeki Müren, legendary voice of Turkish art music known as the 'Sun of Art', spent his last years. His stage costumes, records, photographs, awards, personal belongings and car are displayed in rooms kept as he lived – an emotional stop for a figure cherished in Türkiye's cultural memory.
