Köprülü Kanyon National Park

Köprülü Canyon National Park in Antalya's Manavgat district protects the gorge cut by the Köprüçay river through the Taurus Mountains and the forests around it. One of Türkiye's best-known rafting runs is here; trips go with licensed operators, helmets and life jackets. The Roman Oluk Bridge over the canyon is still in use. Combine it with the nearby ancient city of Selge and the cedar forests.
