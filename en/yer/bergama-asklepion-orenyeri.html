Bergama Asklepion Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › İzmir

🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Asclepieion of Pergamon, dedicated to the healing god Asclepius, was one of the most famous therapeutic centres of the ancient world. Patients used sacred springs and were treated with baths, diet, music and theatre; interpreting dreams was an important part of healing. The celebrated physician Galen of Pergamon drew on this tradition and worked here. Visitors see the sacred way, colonnaded courts, a small theatre and the covered underground corridor to the treatment building. UNESCO World Heritage as part of Pergamon's landscape since 2014.
