Alanya Müzesi

Home › Mediterranean › Antalya
Location Alanya, AntalyaRegion MediterraneanType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Alanya Museum, at the foot of the castle, displays finds from the town and nearby ancient cities. Lying on the border between Cilicia and Pamphylia, the area has a continuous history from the Bronze Age through Roman, Byzantine and Seljuk times. A highlight is an elegant Roman bronze statue of Herakles, while pottery, coins, inscriptions and ethnographic items tell of the town's layered identity. Architectural pieces and sarcophagi fill the garden.
