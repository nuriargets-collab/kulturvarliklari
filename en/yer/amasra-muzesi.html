Amasra Müzesi

Home › Black Sea › Bartın

Amasra Museum tells the long story of one of the Black Sea's loveliest coastal towns. Known in antiquity as Sesamos, it was refounded in the Hellenistic period by the Persian noblewoman Amastris, after whom it is named. An important port under Rome and Byzantium, it became a Genoese trading base in the Middle Ages and joined the Ottoman realm in the 15th century. Roman sculpture, gravestones, coins, pottery and Genoese-era finds are on display.
