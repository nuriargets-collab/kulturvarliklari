Alinda Archaeological Site

Alinda Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › Aydın

Alinda, on a hill above Karpuzlu, surprises with its views and well-preserved buildings. Ancient sources say the Carian queen Ada welcomed Alexander the Great here and adopted him. Its most impressive structure is a multi-storey market building about a hundred metres long, set against the slope, showing how ancient trade worked. Climbing higher, visitors find a theatre, a watchtower and aqueduct remains, with a panorama over the Karpuzlu plain.
