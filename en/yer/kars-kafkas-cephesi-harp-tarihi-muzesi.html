Kars Caucasian Front War History Museum tells of the battles on the Caucasian Front in eastern Anatolia during the First World War and their impact on local people. One of the most tragic events was the Sarıkamış operation in the winter of 1914–15, when tens of thousands of soldiers died in brutal winter conditions – a deep scar in Türkiye's collective memory. Weapons, uniforms, documents, maps and photographs convey the course of the war and the soldiers' hardships.
