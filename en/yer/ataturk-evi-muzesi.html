Atatürk Evi Müzesi

The Diyarbakır Atatürk Pavilion (Atatürk House Museum) is an elegant building among gardens on the banks of the Tigris, near the historic Ten-Eyed Bridge. Presented to Atatürk by the people of Diyarbakır, it recalls his visit to the city. Its façade of black basalt and white stone shows the character of local architecture; photographs and period objects are displayed inside. With the Hevsel Gardens and the Tigris, it reveals Diyarbakır's blend of nature and history.
