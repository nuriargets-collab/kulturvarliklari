Myra Örenyeri

Home › Mediterranean › Antalya
Location Demre, AntalyaRegion MediterraneanType Archaeological sitePeriod Likya / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Myra is one of the most striking cities of Lycia, famous for tombs carved into the cliffs at the edge of the Demre plain. Their façades imitate wooden houses turned to stone, stacked up the rock face in a breathtaking display. Below lies a large Roman theatre with carved fragments of its stage building. Myra also matters in Christian history: Saint Nicholas was bishop here, and its harbour Andriake was an important stop in the Roman grain trade. Come early for cool air and good light.
