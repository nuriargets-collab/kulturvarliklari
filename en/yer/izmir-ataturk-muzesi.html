İzmir Atatürk Müzesi

Home › Aegean › İzmir
Location Konak, İzmirRegion AegeanType MuseumPeriod Cumhuriyet
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

İzmir Atatürk Museum is set in an elegant 19th-century mansion on the Kordon waterfront facing the bay. Atatürk stayed here on his visits to İzmir, and the house was presented to him in the early Republic. With Levantine-influenced architecture, high-ceilinged rooms and sea views, it also reflects the city's cosmopolitan past. Furniture, personal belongings, photographs and documents are displayed – a meaningful stop on a sunset stroll along the Kordon.
