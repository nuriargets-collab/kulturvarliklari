Muğla Müzesi

Location Menteşe, MuğlaRegion AegeanType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Muğla Museum, in the provincial centre, presents the region from prehistory to the Ottomans. A distinctive section shows fossils of animals that lived millions of years ago, while gladiator stelae from Stratonikeia, Carian sculpture, coins and pottery are also notable. The museum lies near Muğla's old quarter of whitewashed historic houses.
