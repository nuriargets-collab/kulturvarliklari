The Monastery of St Simeon, on a high mountain near Samandağ overlooking Antakya and the Orontes valley, was founded in memory of Simeon the Younger, who lived in the 6th century. Simeon was one of the 'stylite' monks who spent years on top of a column to withdraw from the world; the base of his column still stands at the centre of the octagonal court. Churches, pilgrim lodgings and cisterns around it show how this devotion drew thousands. The road up rewards with history and wide views.
