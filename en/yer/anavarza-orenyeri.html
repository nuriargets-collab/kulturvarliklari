Anavarza Örenyeri

Home › Mediterranean › Adana
Location Kozan, AdanaRegion MediterraneanType Archaeological sitePeriod Roma / Bizans
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Anavarza, ancient Anazarbus, lies at the foot of a steep rock that rises abruptly from the Çukurova plain. Important in Roman times, it became one of the capitals of Cilicia in late antiquity and later flourished again under the Armenian Kingdom of Cilicia. Visitors are greeted by a monumental triple-arched gate, while aqueduct ruins show how water was brought from far away. The fortress on top of the rock offers sweeping views and walls from many periods. Earthquakes destroyed much of the city, yet its scattered ruins still reveal its former wealth.
