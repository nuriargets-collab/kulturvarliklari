Anavarza Archaeological Site

Anavarza, ancient Anazarbus, lies at the foot of a steep rock that rises abruptly from the Çukurova plain. Important in Roman times, it became one of the capitals of Cilicia in late antiquity and later flourished again under the Armenian Kingdom of Cilicia. Visitors are greeted by a monumental triple-arched gate, while aqueduct ruins show how water was brought from far away. The fortress on top of the rock offers sweeping views and walls from many periods. Earthquakes destroyed much of the city, yet its scattered ruins still reveal its former wealth.

Visitor guide

🚌 Getting there

Anavarza is in Dilekkaya in the Kozan district of Adana, about 70 km from Adana and 25 km from Kozan. Public transport is limited; a car is recommended.

⭐ Don't miss

  • The monumental triple-arched triumphal gate
  • The colonnaded street
  • Rock-cut tombs and the stadium site
  • The Roman aqueducts across the plain
  • The multi-period castle and cisterns on top of the rock
  • Open-air mosaics in the village

🏺 Finds and artworks

  • Some mosaics found at Anazarbus (for example the "Thetis and Oceanus" mosaic) are displayed in protected areas in the village and in the Adana Museum

👤 Notable people

  • One of the capitals of Cilicia in Roman times
  • The ancient physician and pharmacologist Dioscorides and the poet Oppian are known as natives of Anazarbus
  • It later became a centre of the Armenian Kingdom of Cilicia

💡 Tips

The climb to the castle is long and steep; start in the cool of the morning and bring water. The site is large and shadeless. Can be combined with Yılan Castle and Kozan Castle.
