Çavuşin Örenyeri (Çavuşin Kilisesi)

Home › Central Anatolia › Nevşehir
Location Avanos, NevşehirRegion Central AnatoliaType Archaeological sitePeriod BizansUNESCO 1985
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Çavuşin, between Göreme and Avanos, is an old Cappadocian village of rock houses carved into a cliff, above which stands the Church of St John the Baptist, thought one of Cappadocia's oldest. By the road, the Çavuşin Church (Church of Nicephorus Phocas) is famed for 10th-century frescoes believed to commemorate Emperor Nicephorus II Phocas and his family. Walks among abandoned rock houses and the nearby Rose and Red valleys are magical at sunset.
