Adıyaman Müzesi

Adıyaman Museum presents the civilisations that lived along the Euphrates in this region. Rescue excavations carried out before the large dam projects uncovered thousands of years of settlement, and many of these finds are now on display. Prehistoric tools, Bronze Age pottery and above all sculptures, stelae and inscriptions from the Kingdom of Commagene are highlights. A visit before or after Mount Nemrut helps visitors understand Commagene's blend of Eastern and Western cultures.
