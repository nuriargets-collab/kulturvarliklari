Milas Taş Eserler Müzesi

Milas Stone Works Museum, in ancient Mylasa – an important Carian city, first capital of the Hecatomnid dynasty and long known for its marble quarries – displays sarcophagi, gravestones, inscriptions, architectural fragments and sculpture indoors and out; best combined with the Baltalı Gate and the Gümüşkesen tomb.
