Nymphaion (Nif) Laskaris Palace

Nymphaion (Nif) Laskaris Sarayı

The Nymphaion (Nif) Palace in Kemalpaşa is a four-storey building erected in the 13th century as a winter palace of the Laskarid dynasty of the Empire of Nicaea. Emperor John III Vatatzes often stayed here, and the famous Treaty of Nymphaeum with Genoa was signed here in 1261. Its banded brick-and-stone walls, now under a protective roof after restoration, are a rare example of Byzantine palace architecture in Anatolia.
