İzmir Kültür Sanat Fabrikası

Home › Aegean › İzmir
Location Konak, İzmirRegion AegeanType MuseumPeriod Cumhuriyet / Çağdaş
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

İzmir Culture and Art Factory is a cultural centre created by converting a historic building from the city's industrial heritage. Vast halls once used for production now host exhibitions, art events and museum displays; high ceilings, brick walls and industrial details create an interesting dialogue with the works. Such projects preserve the memory of past working life while giving buildings a new role. Check the current programme before visiting.
