Divriği Ulu Camii ve Darüşşifası

The Great Mosque and Hospital of Divriği, in the Divriği district of Sivas, was built in 1228–1229 by the Mengujekid ruler Ahmed Shah and his wife Turan Melek Sultan. The adjoining mosque and hospital are famous above all for the unparalleled, almost lace-like three-dimensional carving of their stone portals, earning it the nickname 'the Alhambra of Anatolia'. In 1985 it was among the first sites from Türkiye inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List. At certain hours, shadows falling on the portal carvings form figures that fascinate visitors. The mosque section is in use for worship.
