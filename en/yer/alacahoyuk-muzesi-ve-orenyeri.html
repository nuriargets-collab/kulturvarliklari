Alacahöyük Müzesi ve Örenyeri

Alacahöyük, near the Hittite capital Hattusa, is one of Anatolia's most important Bronze Age centres. Excavations begun by Turkish archaeologists in the early Republic uncovered extraordinarily rich royal tombs of the pre-Hittite Hattian culture; their sun discs and stag figurines, now in Ankara's Museum of Anatolian Civilizations, have become symbols of Anatolian art. The site is entered through the monumental Sphinx Gate, whose reliefs show scenes of Hittite ceremonial life. A site museum tells the excavation story.
