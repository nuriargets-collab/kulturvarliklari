Fethiye Tlos Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › Muğla
Location Seydikemer, MuğlaRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Tunç Çağı – Osmanlı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Tlos, near Fethiye, sits on a high crag commanding the Xanthos valley and is considered one of the oldest and largest Lycian cities, with roots possibly in the Bronze Age. Rock tombs on the acropolis slopes include one with a relief of the hero Bellerophon on winged Pegasus, and the summit bears an Ottoman-era fort. A theatre, stadium, baths and agora also survive, close to Saklıkent Gorge.
