Diyarbakır Arkeoloji Müzesi

Diyarbakır Archaeological Museum lies among restored buildings in the city's historic İçkale (inner citadel) and tells tens of thousands of years of history of the upper Tigris basin. The region includes Çayönü, one of the most important Neolithic settlements, whose finds illuminate humanity's first steps towards farming. Works from the Bronze Age, Assyrian, Urartian, Roman, Byzantine, Artuqid, Akkoyunlu and Ottoman periods follow chronologically. The view from İçkale links the visit with the UNESCO-listed walls and Hevsel Gardens.
