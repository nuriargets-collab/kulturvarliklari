Taşucu Atatürk House

Taşucu Atatürk Evi

The Taşucu Atatürk House is a museum opened in 2005 as a faithful replica of the house in Thessaloniki where Atatürk was born. Atatürk visited Taşucu four times, and this is the third such replica built in Turkey after those in Ankara and Istanbul. Inside are period furnishings, photographs and documents about his ties to the area.
