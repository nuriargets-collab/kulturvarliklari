Kaman Kalehöyük Arkeoloji Müzesi

Kaman Kalehöyük Archaeological Museum, in the Kaman district of Kırşehir, displays finds from Kalehöyük, excavated for many years by a Japanese team. The mound revealed an unbroken sequence of cultures from the Early Bronze Age to the Ottoman period, making it a reference point for Anatolian chronology. The modern building, built with Japanese support, and the adjacent Japanese garden symbolise scientific and cultural cooperation between the two countries.
