Kibyra Örenyeri

Home › Mediterranean › Burdur
Location Gölhisar, BurdurRegion MediterraneanType Archaeological sitePeriod Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Kibyra, near Gölhisar in Burdur, sits on a high plateau and was famed in antiquity for its ironwork. Destroyed by a great earthquake in the 1st century AD, it was rebuilt with imperial support. A highlight is the odeon, whose floor before the stage is decorated with a magnificent coloured-stone Medusa mosaic. A stadium overlooking the valley, theatre, agora and colonnaded streets reveal a regional power, now one of the Lake District's most impressive sites.
