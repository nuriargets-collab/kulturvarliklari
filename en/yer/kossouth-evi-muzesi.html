Kossouth Evi Müzesi

Kossuth House Museum is the house in Kütahya where Lajos Kossuth, leader of the 1848 Hungarian Revolution, lived after taking refuge in the Ottoman Empire when the revolution was crushed. Despite pressure from Austria and Russia, the Ottomans refused to hand him and his companions over – a key symbol of Turkish-Hungarian friendship. A fine traditional Kütahya house, it displays documents, photographs and period objects on his life in exile.
