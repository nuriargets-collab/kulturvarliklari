Malazgirt Müzesi

Home › Eastern Anatolia › Muş
Location Malazgirt, MuşRegion Eastern AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Selçuklu (1071)
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Malazgirt Museum tells the story of the Battle of Manzikert in 1071 between the Seljuk Sultan Alp Arslan and the Byzantine Emperor Romanos Diogenes, regarded as opening Anatolia to the Turks. The background, the two armies, weapons and consequences are explained with models, maps, reconstructions and local finds; best combined with Malazgirt Castle and the commemoration ground used each August.
