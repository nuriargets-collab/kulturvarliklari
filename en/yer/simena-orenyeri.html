Simena Örenyeri

Home › Mediterranean › Antalya
Location Demre, AntalyaRegion MediterraneanType Archaeological sitePeriod Likya / Bizans

Simena, in the Kekova area above today's Kaleköy, is a small but striking Lycian settlement. Its medieval castle offers superb views over turquoise coves and islands, and inside it lies a tiny rock-cut theatre seating only a few hundred – one of Lycia's most charming structures. Lycian sarcophagi on the shore and in the shallows recall settlements partly submerged by earthquakes. Simena is usually reached by boat or kayak, passing Kekova's 'sunken city' ruins.
