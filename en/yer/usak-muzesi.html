Uşak Müzesi

Home › Aegean › Uşak

🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Uşak Museum is known for the Lydian 'Croesus Treasure' of the 6th century BC – gold and silver vessels and jewellery illegally dug from local tumuli, taken abroad and returned to Türkiye after long legal struggles. Its emblem, a winged seahorse brooch, is among the museum's most famous pieces. Finds from prehistory to Byzantium complete a powerful story about protecting and repatriating heritage.
