Kariye Camii (Khora)

Home › Marmara › İstanbul
Location Fatih, İstanbulRegion MarmaraType Religious buildingPeriod Byzantine / Ottoman

The Kariye Mosque is the church of the former Chora Monastery near Istanbul's land walls. Its mosaics and frescoes, commissioned in the early 14th century by the Byzantine statesman Theodore Metochites, rank among the finest examples of late Byzantine art in the world; scenes from the lives of the Virgin and Christ and the Anastasis fresco in the side chapel are especially famous. The building became a mosque in the early 16th century, served as a museum from 1945 and reopened for worship in 2024. As visiting arrangements and hours may change, check before you go.
