Ürgüp Müzesi

Home › Central Anatolia › Nevşehir
Location Ürgüp, NevşehirRegion Central AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Ürgüp Museum, in one of Cappadocia's liveliest tourist centres, presents the region's archaeological and ethnographic heritage: pottery, sculpture, coins and grave finds from prehistory through Hittite, Phrygian, Roman and Byzantine times, and household items, costumes and crafts reflecting traditional life in Ürgüp's stone mansions.
