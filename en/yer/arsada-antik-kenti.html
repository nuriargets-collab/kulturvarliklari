Arsada Antik Kenti

Arsada is a Lycian city on a hill above the Eşen valley near Arsaköy in Seydikemer, between Tlos and Xanthos. On the slopes of Akdağ, it has polygonal walls, Lycian-type sarcophagi, rock tombs and the remains of a Byzantine church. With views over the valley and snowy peaks, it is a striking stop in the Lycian interior.
