Midas Yazılıkaya Örenyeri

Midas City (Yazılıkaya), in the Han district of Eskişehir at the heart of the Phrygian Valley, is a rock settlement with some of the most impressive Phrygian monuments. Its emblem is a rock-cut façade about seventeen metres high, decorated with geometric patterns; because a Phrygian inscription on it mentions Midas, it is known as the Midas Monument. Altars, stepped cult areas, tunnels to cisterns and rock tombs cover the plateau, glowing warmly in afternoon light.
