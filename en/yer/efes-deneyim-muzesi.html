The Ephesus Experience Museum is a centre that brings the city's past to life with digital technology before or after a visit to the ruins. Through audio-visual installations and reconstructions, visitors travel through Ephesus's story from its founding to its Roman splendour, from the Temple of Artemis to harbour life. A helpful complement for those – especially children – who find it hard to imagine how the ruins once looked.
