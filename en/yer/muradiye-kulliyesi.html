Muradiye Complex

Muradiye Külliyesi

The Muradiye Complex in Bursa, built by Murad II in 1425–1426, comprises a mosque, madrasa and bath. Its fame comes from the twelve tombs set among rose gardens: those of Murad II, princes and royal consorts, decorated with tiles and painted ornament, are known as the "cemetery of the Ottoman dynasty". Part of the UNESCO World Heritage site.
