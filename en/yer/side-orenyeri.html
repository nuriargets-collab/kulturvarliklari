Side Örenyeri

Home › Mediterranean › Antalya
Location Manavgat, AntalyaRegion MediterraneanType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Side was built on a narrow peninsula and is now a special place where ancient ruins and a modern resort town coexist. Its name is thought to mean 'pomegranate' in the local language. An important trading port in antiquity, it is crowned by a large theatre built largely on arched substructures rather than against a hillside. At the tip of the peninsula, the columns of the temples of Apollo and Athena facing the sea form one of the Mediterranean's most photographed sunsets.
