Kütahya Tile Museum, in a historic soup-kitchen building from the Germiyanid period, presents the city's centuries-old tile and ceramic tradition. With İznik, Kütahya was one of the two great centres of Ottoman tile art, and the tradition lives on in workshops today. Tiles and ceramics from the Seljuk era to the present – blue-and-white wares, colourful plates, lamps and wall tiles – show changing colours, patterns and techniques. Visiting the city's workshops afterwards shows how the art is made.
