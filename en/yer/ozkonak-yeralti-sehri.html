Özkonak Yeraltı Şehri

Home › Central Anatolia › Nevşehir
Location Avanos, NevşehirRegion Central AnatoliaType Underground cityPeriod Bizans
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Özkonak Underground City, in Özkonak north of Avanos, is carved into a hillside and known for small holes linking its levels, thought to have served for both ventilation and communication. Rolling stones in the passages and holes interpreted as for pouring hot liquid on attackers reveal clever defences; fewer crowds make for a relaxed visit.
