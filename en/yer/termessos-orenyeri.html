Termessos Örenyeri

Home › Mediterranean › Antalya
Location Döşemealtı, AntalyaRegion MediterraneanType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Termessos, north-west of Antalya, is a Pisidian city built high on the steep slopes of Mount Güllük and almost swallowed by nature. Its inaccessible position made it one of antiquity's best-defended places; ancient writers say even Alexander the Great besieged it but had to move on. Now within a national park, its sarcophagi, cisterns, gymnasium and council house lie scattered among trees and rocks. The highlight is the theatre on the cliff edge, overlooking the Taurus and the Antalya plain. Comfortable shoes and water are essential.
