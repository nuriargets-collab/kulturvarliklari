Rize Atatürk Evi Müzesi

Home › Black Sea › Rize
Location Merkez, RizeRegion Black SeaType MuseumPeriod Cumhuriyet
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Rize Atatürk House Museum is a historic villa on the hillside where Atatürk spent a night during his 1924 visit. Built in the early 20th century, it is an elegant blend of Black Sea timber architecture and European influences, with his room, period furniture and photographs; its garden overlooks the city, tea gardens and the sea.
