Ordu Müzesi

Location Altınordu, OrduRegion Black SeaType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli

Ordu Museum occupies the Paşaoğlu Mansion, a late 19th-century house whose architecture reflects the life of wealthy families in Black Sea towns. Rooms furnished in period style, local costumes, jewellery and household items are shown alongside pottery, coins and small finds from ancient Kotyora and the area.
