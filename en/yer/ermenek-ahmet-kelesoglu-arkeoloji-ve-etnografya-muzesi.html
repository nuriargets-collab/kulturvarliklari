Ermenek Ahmet Keleşoğlu Arkeoloji ve Etnografya Müzesi

Ermenek Ahmet Keleşoğlu Archaeology and Ethnography Museum presents the past of Ermenek, set among the deep valleys of the Taurus near the headwaters of the Göksu. In the Middle Ages Ermenek was an important centre of the Karamanid principality. Archaeological finds from ancient settlements are shown with local costumes, crafts and everyday objects – a different experience in the heart of the Taurus.
