Kemer Köprüsü (Seydikemer)

The Kemer Bridge is the remains of a Roman arch bridge over the broad bed of the Eşen (Xanthos) river, about 4 km upstream from Kemer village in Seydikemer. Of a crossing that once spanned a riverbed nearly 500 metres wide, a section of about 29 metres still stands. Set in the green landscape of the Xanthos valley, it is a quiet witness to Lycia's ancient road network.
