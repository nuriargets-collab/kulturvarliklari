Aşıklı Höyük

Home › Central Anatolia › Aksaray
Location Merkez, AksarayRegion Central AnatoliaType Archaeological sitePeriod Neolitik
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Aşıklı Höyük, on the Melendiz stream, is one of the oldest known villages in Central Anatolia. Some ten thousand years ago its people took the first steps from hunting and gathering towards settled life, building adjoining mud-brick houses and experimenting with the management of sheep and goats. Houses were often entered from the roof and the dead buried beneath floors. Reconstructed houses at the site help visitors imagine the world of the first farmers.
