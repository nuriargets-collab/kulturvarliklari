Rumeli Hisarı

Home › Marmara › İstanbul
Location Sarıyer, İstanbulRegion MarmaraType Castle & historic buildingPeriod Osmanlı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Rumeli Hisarı is a magnificent fortress built at one of the narrowest points of the Bosphorus by Sultan Mehmed the Conqueror in 1452, in just a few months, a year before the conquest of Istanbul. With Anadolu Hisarı opposite, it was meant to control passage and cut Byzantium off from help by sea. Its three great towers are named after the viziers who supervised their construction, and walls run down the slope to the water. Inside are green spaces and an open-air theatre that hosts summer concerts; the views of the Bosphorus are among the finest.
