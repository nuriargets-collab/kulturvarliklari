Kastabala Örenyeri

Home › Mediterranean › Osmaniye
Location Merkez, OsmaniyeRegion MediterraneanType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma / Ortaçağ
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Kastabala (Hierapolis-Kastabala), near Osmaniye on the Ceyhan plain, was a Cilician city known for its sanctuary of the goddess Perasia, whose priestesses were said to walk over glowing coals. A long colonnaded street, theatre, baths, church remains and an impressive medieval castle on the hill can be seen – especially photogenic in spring. As the region was affected by the 2023 earthquakes, check current opening status before visiting.
