Alahan Monastery

Alahan Manastırı

Alahan Monastery, in Mut on Taurus slopes above the Göksu valley, is a 5th-century monastic complex counted among the most impressive examples of early Byzantine architecture. Stretched along a rocky terrace, it comprises churches, a baptistery, cave rooms and colonnaded passages. Figures symbolising the four Evangelists on the West Church door frame and the fine stonework of the East Church reflect the art of the period, while the valley views make it unforgettable.

Visitor guide

🚌 Getting there

Alahan Monastery is in the Mut district of Mersin, at the end of a side road off the Mut–Karaman highway, on a slope above the Göksu valley about 20 km from Mut. A car is needed.

⭐ Don't miss

  • The churches and baptistery along the rocky terrace
  • The carved doorway of the West Church with symbols of the four Evangelists
  • The well-preserved architecture of the East Church
  • Rock-cut rooms
  • Views over the Göksu valley

👤 Notable people

  • A monastic complex founded in the 5th century
  • Counted among the most impressive examples of early Byzantine architecture

💡 Tips

The road is winding but the views are worth it. The site is small; an hour is enough. Combine with the Lal Pasha Mosque and Mut Castle in town.
