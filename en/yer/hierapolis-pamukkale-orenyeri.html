Hierapolis (Pamukkale) Örenyeri

Hierapolis sits just above the brilliant white travertines of Pamukkale – one of the rare places where nature and history merge so completely. Founded by the kings of Pergamon in the 2nd century BC, it became famous in Roman times as a spa thanks to its hot springs. Terraces built by calcium-rich water over centuries cover the slopes like a cotton castle. Visitors walk the main street, monumental gates, a grand theatre with a re-erected stage building and one of Anatolia's largest ancient cemeteries. The Martyrium of St Philip recalls its Christian history, and the Antique Pool, with sunken columns, is a delightful break. UNESCO World Heritage since 1988.
