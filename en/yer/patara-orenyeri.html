Patara Örenyeri

Home › Mediterranean › Antalya
Location Kaş, AntalyaRegion MediterraneanType Archaeological sitePeriod Likya / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Patara was one of Lycia's most important ports and lies just behind its famous dune-backed beach, which stretches for kilometres. Its most remarkable building is the assembly hall where representatives of the Lycian League met; carefully restored, it is often cited as an early example of representative government. A Roman lighthouse, a monumental city gate, a theatre and the main street show Patara's wealth. Known as the birthplace of Saint Nicholas, it also has a beach where protected sea turtles nest, so it closes in the evenings.
