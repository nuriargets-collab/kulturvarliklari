Sakarya Museum in Adapazarı occupies an early 20th-century mansion and new exhibition buildings. Prehistoric finds, Roman and Byzantine sculpture, gravestones, coins and glass are shown; monuments such as the Justinian Bridge recall the region's role as a crossing point, while the ethnography section reflects the diversity of communities who settled here.
