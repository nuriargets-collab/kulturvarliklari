Kars Müzesi

Kars Museum presents thousands of years of history of the Kars region, a gateway between the Caucasus and Anatolia. It saw the Karaz culture in the Early Bronze Age, the Urartians in the Iron Age and, in the Middle Ages, the Bagratids, Seljuks and other Turkish states. Finds from Ani, pottery, metalwork and coins are shown with Kars's famous carpets and kilims, costumes and everyday objects – good preparation before visiting Ani.
