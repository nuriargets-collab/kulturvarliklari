Sivas Atatürk ve Kongre Müzesi

Home › Central Anatolia › Sivas
Location Merkez, SivasRegion Central AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Cumhuriyet (1919)
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Sivas Atatürk and Congress Museum occupies an 1892 school building where the Sivas Congress met on 4–11 September 1919 – a turning point that organised the national struggle across the country. The congress hall and Atatürk's study and bedroom are arranged with period items, photographs, documents and maps.
