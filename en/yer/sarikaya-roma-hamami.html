Sarıkaya Roma Hamamı

Home › Central Anatolia › Yozgat

Sarıkaya Roman Bath (Basilica Therma), in Sarıkaya, Yozgat, lies over hot springs used since Roman times. An important spa in antiquity for its healing waters and still used in the Byzantine period, its large pools lined with cut stone, steps and arched structures show how Roman engineers managed water; locally called the 'King's Daughter's Bath'.
