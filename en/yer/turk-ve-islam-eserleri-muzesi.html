Türk ve İslam Eserleri Müzesi

Home › Marmara › İstanbul
Location Fatih, İstanbulRegion MarmaraType MuseumPeriod Selçuklu / Osmanlı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Museum of Turkish and Islamic Arts occupies the İbrahim Pasha Palace on Sultanahmet Square, on the edge of the ancient Hippodrome. Belonging to İbrahim Pasha, grand vizier of Süleyman the Magnificent, it is one of the rare surviving Ottoman palace buildings in Istanbul. Founded in the early 20th century, the museum is regarded as one of the world's most important carpet museums, especially for Seljuk and Ottoman carpets, alongside manuscripts, calligraphy, tiles, wood and metalwork and an ethnography section on nomadic and settled life.
