Kaymaklı Yeraltı Şehri

Home › Central Anatolia › Nevşehir
Location Kaymaklı, NevşehirRegion Central AnatoliaType Underground cityPeriod BizansUNESCO 1985
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Kaymaklı Underground City is, with Derinkuyu, one of Cappadocia's most visited. Its tunnel network spreads beneath the village, connected to house cellars that locals long used as stores and stables. Open levels reveal stables, storerooms, wine presses, kitchens, a small church and ventilation shafts; stooping through narrow passages makes tangible how thousands lived in times of danger.
