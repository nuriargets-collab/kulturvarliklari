Mazı Yeraltı Şehri

Home › Central Anatolia › Nevşehir
Location Ürgüp, NevşehirRegion Central AnatoliaType Underground cityPeriod Bizans
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Mazı Underground City, in Mazı village near Ürgüp, is one of Cappadocia's quieter underground cities. Entered from the valley slope, it holds stables, wineries, storerooms, a church and rolling stones that sealed the passages, with rock tombs and churches around the village – an authentic alternative away from crowds.
