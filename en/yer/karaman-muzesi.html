Karaman Müzesi

Home › Central Anatolia › Karaman
Location Merkez, KaramanRegion Central AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Karaman Museum presents the rich past of Karaman, which gave its name to the Karamanid principality. In prehistory the area had important Chalcolithic settlements such as Canhasan, and the Byzantine sites of 'Binbir Kilise' (Thousand and One Churches) on Karadağ witness its religious history. A famous Karamanid decree on using Turkish as the official language gives the city symbolic importance in the history of the Turkish language. Archaeology, coins and ethnography are displayed.
