Cumhuriyet Müzesi

Home › Central Anatolia › Ankara
Location Altındağ, AnkaraRegion Central AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Cumhuriyet
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Museum of the Republic in Ulus, Ankara, occupies the building used as the second home of the Grand National Assembly of Turkey. Designed by architect Vedat Tek, it shows the stonework, arched windows and wide eaves of the First National Architectural Movement. The Assembly met here during the early decades of the Republic, debating and adopting many founding laws and reforms. Today the assembly hall, documents and photographs preserve the atmosphere of the young Republic's seat of government.
