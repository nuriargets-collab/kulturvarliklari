Atatürk, Redif ve Etnografya Müzesi

Home › Marmara › Kocaeli
Location İzmit, KocaeliRegion MarmaraType MuseumPeriod Osmanlı / Cumhuriyet
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Atatürk, Redif and Ethnography Museum, in one of İzmit's historic buildings, sheds light on the city's recent history and daily culture. 'Redif' was the name for reserve units of the Ottoman army, and the building is linked with this military past. Documents, photographs and objects about Atatürk's visits and the War of Independence in Kocaeli are shown with local costumes, crafts and household items.
