Atlas 1948 İstanbul Sinema Müzesi

Home › Marmara › İstanbul
Location Beyoğlu, İstanbulRegion MarmaraType MuseumPeriod Cumhuriyet / Çağdaş
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Atlas 1948 Istanbul Cinema Museum is housed in the historic building of the Atlas Cinema, one of İstiklal Avenue's legendary theatres. Beyoğlu was the heart of film culture in Türkiye, from the first screenings to the golden age of Yeşilçam, and Atlas was where generations of Istanbulites watched films. The museum shows the development of Turkish cinema through posters, costumes, projectors, set objects and memorabilia of beloved stars.
