Tarsus Museum presents the rich heritage of Tarsus, with thousands of years of continuous settlement. The city is known for prehistoric layers at Gözlükule mound, its importance as capital of Cilicia and tales of the meeting of Antony and Cleopatra. Prehistoric finds, Roman sculpture, mosaics, sarcophagi, coins and jewellery are displayed with local ethnography – best combined with Cleopatra's Gate, the Roman road and old houses.
