The Tomb of Akşemseddin in the historic town of Göynük, Bolu, holds the grave of the scholar and mystic Akşemseddin, known as the teacher of Sultan Mehmed the Conqueror. He offered spiritual support to the young sultan during the conquest of Istanbul and was renowned for his learning in medicine, religion and Sufism. The tomb is a fine example of plain, balanced early Ottoman architecture within Göynük's well-preserved timber-house townscape.
