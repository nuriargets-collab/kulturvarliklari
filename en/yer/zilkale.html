Zilkale

Zilkale is a medieval fortress rising among lush forests on the slope of the Fırtına valley in Çamlıhemşin, Rize. Dating to the Byzantine or Trebizond period, it looks like something from a fairy tale on misty days. One of the most photogenic sights of the Black Sea, it is a stop on the way to the Ayder and Palovit plateaus.
