Antalya Nekropol Müzesi

Antalya Necropolis Museum focuses on the culture of death and remembrance in the Antalya region in antiquity. In the cities of Pamphylia, Lycia and Pisidia, people honoured loved ones with richly carved sarcophagi, inscribed gravestones and monumental tombs – documents of status, belief and daily life. The museum explains funerary architecture, burial customs and grave goods, helping visitors decode the sarcophagi they see along ancient roads.
