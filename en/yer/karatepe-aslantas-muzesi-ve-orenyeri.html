Karatepe-Aslantaş, in Kadirli, Osmaniye, is a Late Hittite fortress and open-air museum on a wooded hill above the Aslantaş reservoir on the Ceyhan. Founded in the 8th century BC by a ruler named Azatiwada, its gates are lined with carved slabs of people, animals, banquets and hunts. Bilingual inscriptions in Phoenician and Luwian hieroglyphs found here were key to deciphering Luwian hieroglyphic script; many works are displayed in place. As the region was affected by the 2023 earthquakes, check current opening status before visiting.
