Karahantepe Örenyeri

Home › Southeastern Anatolia › Şanlıurfa
Location Haliliye, ŞanlıurfaRegion Southeastern AnatoliaType Archaeological sitePeriod Neolitik
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Karahantepe, in the Tektek mountains east of Şanlıurfa, is a Neolithic site contemporary with Göbeklitepe, about 11,000 years old, and one of the most remarkable sites in the 'Taş Tepeler' (Stone Hills) research project covering many nearby Neolithic sites. In a rock-cut structure, a human head emerging from the wall and surrounding phallic pillars reveal a rich symbolic world, prompting us to rethink the transition to settled life. Şanlıurfa was among the provinces affected by the 2023 earthquakes; check current status before visiting.
