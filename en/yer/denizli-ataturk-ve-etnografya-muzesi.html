Denizli Atatürk ve Etnografya Müzesi

Home › Aegean › Denizli
Location Merkezefendi, DenizliRegion AegeanType MuseumPeriod Cumhuriyet
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Denizli Atatürk and Ethnography Museum is set in a historic house where Atatürk stayed during his visit to Denizli. Upstairs, photographs, documents and period-furnished rooms recall his stay; downstairs, ethnographic items from the region are displayed. Denizli has been known for weaving for centuries, and handlooms, textiles, local costumes and jewellery reflect this tradition.
