Çorum Müzesi

Çorum Museum lies in the heartland of Hittite civilisation and presents finds from major Hittite centres such as Hattusa, Alacahöyük, Şapinuva and Ortaköy. It is housed in a historic early 20th-century hospital building. Hittite pottery, tablets, weapons, seals and cult vessels are highlights, alongside works from the Chalcolithic to the Ottoman period – an ideal overview before visiting the Hittite capitals.
