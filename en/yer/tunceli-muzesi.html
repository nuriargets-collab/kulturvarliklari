Tunceli Müzesi

Home › Eastern Anatolia › Tunceli
Location Merkez, TunceliRegion Eastern AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Tunceli Museum presents the cultural and historical heritage of Tunceli, formerly Dersim, ringed by the Munzur mountains and valleys and known for its distinctive beliefs and traditions. Archaeological finds are shown with local costumes, weavings, household items and crafts – a good start before exploring Munzur Valley National Park.
