Sofraz Anıt Mezarı

Home › Southeastern Anatolia › Adıyaman
Location Merkez, AdıyamanRegion Southeastern AnatoliaType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Sofraz Monumental Tomb is one of the examples of the rich funerary tradition of the Adıyaman region, reaching back to Commagenian and Roman times. Powerful families and rulers here honoured their dead with imposing tombs and mounds, which also served as lasting symbols of lineage and power. Set in a quiet rural landscape, it complements the better-known sites of Mount Nemrut and Arsameia. Visitors are advised to check current access with the local museum directorate.
