Aizanoi Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › Kütahya
Location Çavdarhisar, KütahyaRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Aizanoi, in Çavdarhisar near Kütahya on the Penkalas stream, was one of Phrygia's richest cities in Roman times. Its emblem is the Temple of Zeus, one of the best-preserved Roman temples in Anatolia, with a vaulted underground chamber open to visitors. Another unique structure combines a stadium and theatre in a single complex. The round market building bears the inscription of Emperor Diocletian's edict fixing prices – a unique document of the ancient economy – and Roman bridges still in use cross the stream.
