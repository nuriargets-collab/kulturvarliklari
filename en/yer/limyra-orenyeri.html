Limyra Örenyeri

Home › Mediterranean › Antalya
Location Finike, AntalyaRegion MediterraneanType Archaeological sitePeriod Likya / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Limyra, near Finike, is an enchanting Lycian city where spring water from the Taurus flows among the ruins. Capital of the Lycian ruler Pericles in the 4th century BC, it is known for his heroon on the acropolis slope, whose reliefs are now in Antalya Museum. Many rock tombs, a Roman theatre and Byzantine buildings enrich the site; Gaius Caesar, grandson of Augustus, died here and was honoured with a monument. Turtles swimming in the channels make it one of Lycia's most peaceful sites.
