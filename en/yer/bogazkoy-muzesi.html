Boğazköy Müzesi

Home › Black Sea › Çorum
Location Boğazkale, ÇorumRegion Black SeaType MuseumPeriod Hitit
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Boğazköy Museum, close to the Hattusa site, displays finds from the Hittite capital. Its most striking pieces are the sphinx statues that once adorned one of the city gates and returned to Türkiye after many years abroad; their reunion is seen as a symbolic moment for heritage protection. Cuneiform tablets, seal impressions, pottery and metalwork reveal details of Hittite administration, trade and religion. Best seen before touring Hattusa.
