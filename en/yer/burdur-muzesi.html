Burdur Müzesi

Home › Mediterranean › Burdur
Location Merkez, BurdurRegion MediterraneanType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Burdur Museum is one of the richest archaeological museums of the Lake District. The area is home to Hacılar, one of Anatolia's earliest farming villages, whose elegant painted pottery and female figurines are important examples of Neolithic and Chalcolithic art. Other stars are the colossal imperial statues and fine marble works from nearby Sagalassos, whose scale shows the grandeur of Roman Pisidian cities.
