Kültepe Örenyeri

Home › Central Anatolia › Kayseri

🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Kültepe, near Kayseri, is a great mound known in antiquity as Kanesh or Nesha and regarded as the place where Anatolia's written history begins. Some four thousand years ago Assyrian merchants founded a trading colony, the 'karum', at its foot, bringing tin and textiles and taking Anatolian silver and gold. Tens of thousands of cuneiform tablets record their family letters, loans, marriage contracts and court cases – one of the world's oldest commercial archives. House foundations and palace ruins can be seen on site.
