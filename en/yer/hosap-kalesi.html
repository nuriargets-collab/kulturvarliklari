Hoşap Kalesi

Home › Eastern Anatolia › Van

🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Hoşap Castle, in Gürpınar, Van, crowns a rocky hill beside the Hoşap stream. Known to have been built in the 17th century by Sarı Süleyman Bey of the Mahmudi, it is noted for lion reliefs and inscriptions over its monumental gateway. Inside are a mosque, palace sections, dungeons and cisterns; with the village below and the historic bridge, it is one of eastern Anatolia's most photogenic castles.
