Havariler (Kümbet) Camii

The Church of the Holy Apostles (Kümbet Mosque), at the foot of Kars Castle, is an Armenian church built in the 10th century by the Bagratid King Abas I. Reliefs of the twelve apostles on the outside of its drum give it its name. Converted into a mosque under the Ottomans and a church under the Russians, it is used as a mosque today.
