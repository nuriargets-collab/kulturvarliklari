Tekirdağ Müzesi

Tekirdağ Museum, on the Marmara shore, occupies an early 20th-century building also known as the Governor's Mansion. Prehistoric finds from Thrace and Thracian, Hellenistic and Roman works are shown; highlights include grave goods, horse harness and ornaments from Thracian tumuli, reflecting Thracian horse culture and burial customs.
