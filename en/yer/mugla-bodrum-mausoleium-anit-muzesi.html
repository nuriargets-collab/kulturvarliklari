The Mausoleum site in ancient Halicarnassus holds the remains of the tomb built in the 4th century BC for the Carian satrap Mausolus – one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World. Completed by his wife and sister Artemisia with the most famous sculptors of the age, it gave the word 'mausoleum' to every language. Destroyed by earthquakes in the Middle Ages, some stones were reused in Bodrum Castle; foundations, architectural fragments and models help imagine its splendour.
