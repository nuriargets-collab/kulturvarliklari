Arsemeia Örenyeri

Home › Southeastern Anatolia › Adıyaman
Location Kahta, AdıyamanRegion Southeastern AnatoliaType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik (Kommagene)
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Arsameia was a sacred site and summer residence of the Commagene kings, set on a slope above the Kahta valley on the road to Mount Nemrut. Its most famous feature is a large relief showing a Commagene king shaking hands with the hero-god Heracles, a powerful statement of the kings' claimed closeness to the gods. Nearby are a long rock-cut tunnel and an extensive Greek inscription describing the kingdom's religious rules. A walk up the hill offers wide views of the valley.
