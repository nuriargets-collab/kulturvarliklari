Fethiye Pınara Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › Muğla
Location Seydikemer, MuğlaRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Likya / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Pınara, near Fethiye, is a Lycian city at the foot of a huge rock face riddled with hundreds of tomb openings. A leading member of the Lycian League, its striking sight is the tomb chambers cut seemingly out of reach high in the cliff. Below lie a theatre, odeon, agora, temple remains and the relief-decorated Royal Tomb, amid pine forest and away from crowds.
