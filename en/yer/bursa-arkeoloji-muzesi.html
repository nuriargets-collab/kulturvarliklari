Bursa Arkeoloji Müzesi

Bursa Archaeological Museum, in the green grounds of Kültürpark, introduces the ancient past of Bithynia and Mysia. Bursa was known in antiquity as Prusa, and nearby cities such as Nicaea (İznik), Apollonia and Kios were centres of the rich life south of the Marmara. Prehistoric finds, Roman sculpture and portraits, gravestones, glass, bronze, jewellery and coins are displayed, with sarcophagi in the garden.
