Mehmet Akif Ersoy Memorial House is a house museum honouring Mehmet Akif Ersoy, poet of the Turkish National Anthem. Born and raised in Istanbul, he joined the national struggle in Anatolia during the War of Independence; the anthem, adopted by the Grand National Assembly in 1921, gave him a special place in Turkish literature and memory. Photographs, documents, books and belongings tell of his life, works and family.
