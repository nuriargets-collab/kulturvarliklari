Klazomenai Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › İzmir
Location Urla, İzmirRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Arkaik
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Klazomenai, on the coast of Urla, was an Ionian city on the islet now called Karantina Island and the shore opposite. Its most fascinating feature is an Archaic-period olive-oil workshop: based on the finds, archaeologists reconstructed the ancient press so visitors can see this historic technology in place. Klazomenai is also famous for painted terracotta sarcophagi with battle and hunting scenes. The philosopher Anaxagoras was born here – a delightful stop for tracing thousands of years of olive-oil culture.
