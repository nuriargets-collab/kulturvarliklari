Antalya Müzesi

Home › Mediterranean › Antalya
Location Muratpaşa, AntalyaRegion MediterraneanType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Antalya Museum is one of Türkiye's most comprehensive archaeological museums, uniting treasures from Pamphylia, Lycia and Pisidia. The tour begins with prehistoric finds from Karain Cave and continues through halls of gods and emperors from Perge, where marble seems to come alive. A highlight is the Weary Herakles, whose two halves were long separated in different countries before being reunited. Richly carved Roman sarcophagi, mosaics, coins and repatriated works complete the collection.
