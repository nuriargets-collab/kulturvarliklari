İslam Bilim ve Teknoloji Tarihi Müzesi

The Museum for the History of Science and Technology in Islam is housed in one of Topkapı Palace's former stable buildings in Gülhane Park. Founded under historian of science Fuat Sezgin, it presents work in astronomy, geography, medicine, chemistry, mathematics, optics and mechanics in the Islamic world between the 8th and 16th centuries. Carefully made replicas of astrolabes, celestial globes, maps, surgical instruments and al-Jazari's automata show how this knowledge passed to Europe and the world. Instructive and fun for children too.
