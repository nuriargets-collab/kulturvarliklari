Samsun Müzesi

Samsun Museum presents Samsun, built over ancient Amisos, and the central Black Sea region. Its most famous pieces are Hellenistic gold jewellery from tumuli at Amisos – finely worked diadems, earrings and necklaces known as the Amisos Treasure. Bronze Age finds from sites such as İkiztepe, Roman mosaics, sculpture and coins complete the collection.
