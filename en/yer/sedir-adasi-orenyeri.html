Sedir Adası Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › Muğla
Location Marmaris, MuğlaRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Sedir Island, in the Gulf of Gökova, holds the remains of ancient Kedreai. It is famed for Cleopatra Beach, whose fine, round, white sand is considered a local formation and is strictly protected. Legend says Antony brought the sand from Egypt for Cleopatra. A theatre, temple, walls and church remains can also be seen; visitors must follow rules to protect the sand.
