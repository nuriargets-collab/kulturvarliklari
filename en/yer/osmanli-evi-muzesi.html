Osmanlı Evi Müzesi

Home › Marmara › Bursa
Location Osmangazi, BursaRegion MarmaraType MuseumPeriod Osmanlı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Ottoman House Museum in Bursa's Muradiye quarter occupies a historic building with the typical features of Ottoman-era Bursa houses: a projecting upper floor, carved wooden ceilings, rooms with built-in divans and a plan linked to an inner garden. Furnished rooms recreate an Ottoman family's daily life. Visited with the nearby Muradiye complex and its princes' tombs, it shows the calm, elegant side of the first Ottoman capital.
