Fethiye Letoon Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › Muğla
Location Seydikemer, MuğlaRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Likya / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Letoon, near Xanthos, was the most important sanctuary of the Lycian League. Three temples to Leto and her children Apollo and Artemis stand side by side; legend says Leto sought refuge here. A trilingual inscription in Lycian, Greek and Aramaic greatly helped decipher Lycian. Inscribed with Xanthos on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1988, the sanctuary with its theatre and partly water-filled nymphaeum offers a peaceful visit among frogs and turtles.
