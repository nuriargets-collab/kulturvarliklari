Kız Kulesi

🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Maiden's Tower rises on a small rock off Üsküdar, where the Bosphorus opens into the Marmara, and is one of Istanbul's most romantic symbols. Use of the rock reaches back to antiquity; over time the tower served as watchtower, lighthouse and quarantine station, and its present look largely comes from Ottoman renovations. Its best-known legend tells of a princess shut away here to escape a prophecy, who could not escape her fate. Reopened as a museum after extensive restoration, it is reached by a short boat ride; the terrace frames the historic peninsula's skyline.
