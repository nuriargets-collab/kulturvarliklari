Mersin Kız Kalesi

Home › Mediterranean › Mersin
Location Erdemli, MersinRegion MediterraneanType Castle & historic buildingPeriod Bizans / Ortaçağ
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Kızkalesi (Maiden's Castle), at Erdemli a few hundred metres offshore, is an enchanting sea castle covering an entire islet. Built in the Middle Ages with the land castle of ancient Korykos opposite to guard the harbour, it was strengthened by the Byzantines and the Armenian Kingdom of Cilicia. Legend says a king, fearing a prophecy that his daughter would die of a snakebite, shut her in this castle – but she could not escape her fate. Reach it by boat or, for strong swimmers, by swimming.
