Sagalassos Örenyeri

Home › Mediterranean › Burdur
Location Ağlasun, BurdurRegion MediterraneanType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Sagalassos, set between 1,400 and 1,700 metres in the Taurus Mountains, is one of the ancient world's most impressive mountain cities. The leading city of Pisidia, it was taken by Alexander the Great and enjoyed its golden age under Rome. Long-running international excavations and restorations have virtually brought it back to life: the Antonine Fountain flows again, and the council house and theatre rise among the mountains. Colossal imperial statues found here are striking proof of its splendour.
