Akdamar Anıt Müzesi

Home › Eastern Anatolia › Van
Location Gevaş, VanRegion Eastern AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Ortaçağ (10. yy)
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Church of the Holy Cross on Akdamar Island, in Lake Van, was built in the early 10th century by King Gagik I of Vaspurakan. Its exterior reliefs are among the finest works of medieval Armenian art: Adam and Eve, Jonah and the whale, David and Goliath, animals, vine scrolls and the king himself. Restored and opened as a museum in 2007, it also hosts an annual service; the boat trip across turquoise water beneath snowy peaks is unforgettable.
