Labraunda Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › Muğla
Location Milas, MuğlaRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Karya / Helenistik
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Labraunda, in the mountains north of Milas amid pine forest, was Caria's most important sanctuary, dedicated to Zeus Labraundos – the name thought to derive from the double axe, labrys, his symbol. Grandly remodelled in the 4th century BC under the Hecatomnid rulers Mausolus and Idrieus, it comprises a temple, banqueting halls (andrones), propylaea and monumental stairways on terraces, with an impressive tomb nearby.
