Nemrut Dağı

Home › Southeastern Anatolia › Adıyaman
Location Kahta, AdıyamanRegion Southeastern AnatoliaType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik (Kommagene)UNESCO 1987
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

At the summit of Mount Nemrut, about 2,100 metres high, stands the sacred tomb that King Antiochus I of Commagene built for himself in the first century BC. He covered the peak with a vast artificial mound of crushed stone and laid out ceremonial terraces to the east and west. Colossal statues of gods, the king, an eagle and a lion line the terraces, the gods bearing both Greek and Persian names. Earthquakes later toppled the heads, creating today's unforgettable scene. Sunrise and sunset on the stone faces are the reason visitors make the long journey. UNESCO World Heritage since 1987.
