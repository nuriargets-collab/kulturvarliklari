Mevlana Müzesi

Home › Central Anatolia › Konya
Location Karatay, KonyaRegion Central AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Selçuklu / Osmanlı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Mevlana Museum in Konya holds the tomb of the great mystic and poet Jalal al-Din Rumi and the lodge of his followers, the Mevlevis. Rumi lived in Konya in the 13th century; his Masnavi and thoughts on tolerance, love and humanity are read worldwide today. The turquoise-tiled Green Dome, Konya's emblem, rises above the tomb. Inside are the cenotaphs of Rumi and his family, the semahane, precious Masnavi manuscripts, musical instruments, dervish garments and cells. Converted into a museum in the early Republic, it welcomes visitors from around the world for the Şeb-i Arus ceremonies each December.
