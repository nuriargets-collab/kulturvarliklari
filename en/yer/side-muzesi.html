Side Müzesi

Home › Mediterranean › Antalya
Location Manavgat, AntalyaRegion MediterraneanType MuseumPeriod Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Side Museum is housed inside a Roman bath building in the heart of ancient Side, so visitors see the finds within the stone walls of an ancient structure. Statues, reliefs, sarcophagi and inscriptions from the excavations show how rich Side was as a port city, and the open-air section displays architectural fragments. Small but carefully arranged, it complements a visit to Side's theatre and seaside temples.
