Muğla Knidos Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › Muğla
Location Datça, MuğlaRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Klasik / Helenistik
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Knidos, at the tip of the Datça peninsula where the Aegean meets the Mediterranean, was a Dorian city with two harbours. It was famed for art and science: Praxiteles' Aphrodite, among the first great nude female statues, is said to have stood here, and the astronomer Eudoxus and Sostratus, architect of the Pharos of Alexandria, are known as Knidians. Theatres, temples, the round podium of the Aphrodite temple and the harbours terrace the slope; the sunsets are unforgettable.
