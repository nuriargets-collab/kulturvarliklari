Kaunos, on the Dalyan river at the border of Caria and Lycia, was an ancient port city. Its unforgettable sight is the temple-fronted Royal Tombs carved into the cliffs across the river. A theatre, baths, basilica, temple terrace and harbour remains can be seen; silting of the harbour contributed to its decline, leaving it far inland today. A boat trip from Dalyan through the reeds is the nicest approach.

Visitor guide

🚌 Getting there

Kaunos is in the Ortaca district of Muğla, on the far bank opposite Dalyan. Cross the Dalyan river from Dalyan by rowing boat and walk a short way, or drive round via the village of Çandır.

⭐ Don't miss

  • The temple-fronted Royal Tombs carved into the cliffs (best seen from Dalyan and from the boat)
  • The theatre
  • The harbour agora and fountain
  • The Byzantine basilica
  • Views of the Dalyan delta from the acropolis

👤 Notable people

  • A port city on the border between Caria and Lycia
  • It was Hellenised under the Carian satrap Mausolus

💡 Tips

Boat tours from Dalyan combine the Royal Tombs, Kaunos, the Köyceğiz mud baths and İztuzu beach. Summer middays are harsh; the climb to the acropolis is steep and shadeless.
