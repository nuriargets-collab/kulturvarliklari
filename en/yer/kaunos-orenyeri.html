Kaunos Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › Muğla
Location Ortaca, MuğlaRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Karya / Helenistik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Kaunos, on the Dalyan river at the border of Caria and Lycia, was an ancient port city. Its unforgettable sight is the temple-fronted Royal Tombs carved into the cliffs across the river. A theatre, baths, basilica, temple terrace and harbour remains can be seen; silting of the harbour contributed to its decline, leaving it far inland today. A boat trip from Dalyan through the reeds is the nicest approach.
