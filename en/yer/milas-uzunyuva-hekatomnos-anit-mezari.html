Milas Uzunyuva (Hekatomnos Anıt Mezarı)

The Hecatomnus Mausoleum, on Uzunyuva hill in Milas, is a 4th-century BC monumental tomb and sanctuary built for Hecatomnus, Carian satrap and father of Mausolus. Revealed in 2010 after illegal digging, the relief-decorated sarcophagus in its underground chamber is a masterpiece of Carian art. Ask the Milas Museum about visiting conditions.
