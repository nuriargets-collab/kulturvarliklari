Kırşehir Müzesi

Home › Central Anatolia › Kırşehir
Location Merkez, KırşehirRegion Central AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Kırşehir Museum presents this long-established Central Anatolian city's past from prehistory to the Ottomans. In the Middle Ages Kırşehir was home to Ahi Evran, regarded as founder of the Ahi guild organisation of craftsmen, and its Cacabey Medrese is known as a 13th-century building where astronomy was taught. Archaeological finds from local mounds, coins and ethnography are displayed.
