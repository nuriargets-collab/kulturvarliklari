Adana Müzesi

Home › Mediterranean › Adana
Location Seyhan, AdanaRegion MediterraneanType MuseumPeriod Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Adana Museum is part of a large museum complex on the banks of the Seyhan River, set in a former factory that once embodied the city's cotton and textile identity. For thousands of years the Çukurova plain was a corridor between Anatolia and Syria: the Hittites knew it as Kizzuwatna, and Romans, Byzantines, the Armenian Kingdom of Cilicia, the Ramazanids and Ottomans all left their mark. Ceramics from ancient mounds, Roman sculpture and sarcophagi, mosaics and coins tell this long story inside an industrial heritage building.
