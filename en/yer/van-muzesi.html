Van Müzesi

Home › Eastern Anatolia › Van
Location Tuşba, VanRegion Eastern AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Urartu / Çok Dönemli
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Van Museum holds one of the world's richest collections of the Urartian civilisation. In its new building are bronze belts, shields, helmets, horse harness, gold jewellery, seals and cuneiform tablets showing advanced Urartian metalwork, along with prehistoric finds and Van ethnography, especially kilims – ideal with visits to Van Fortress, Çavuştepe and Ayanis.
