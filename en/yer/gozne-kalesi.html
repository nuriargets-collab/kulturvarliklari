Gözne Castle, north of Mersin near the cool Gözne plateau in the Taurus, is a medieval fortress perched on a steep rock. Like many Cilician castles, it controlled roads linking the coast and the interior. From its walls, merging with the rock, wide views open over pine-forested valleys towards the coast – a pleasant trip combined with the plateau, a summer escape from Mersin's heat.
