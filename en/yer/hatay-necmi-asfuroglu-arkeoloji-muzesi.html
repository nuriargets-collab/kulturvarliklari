Hatay Necmi Asfuroğlu Arkeoloji Müzesi

Hatay Necmi Asfuroğlu Archaeology Museum in Antakya is a private museum where ancient remains uncovered during construction are preserved and displayed in place. Antakya's layered past means new finds emerge almost everywhere; rather than removing them, this museum built over them, letting visitors view remains and mosaic floors from different periods from walkways above. Given the effects of the 2023 earthquakes, check its current status before visiting.
