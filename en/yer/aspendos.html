Aspendos, on the Köprüçay (ancient Eurymedon), has one of the best-preserved theatres of the ancient world. Built in the 2nd century AD under Marcus Aurelius by the architect Zeno, its stage building stands almost complete and the acoustics of its roughly 15,000 seats still amaze. Used for a time as a caravanserai by the Seljuks, it now hosts events such as the Aspendos Opera and Ballet Festival. A basilica and agora on the hill behind and aqueducts stretching across the plain reveal the city's former size.

🎧 A day in Aspendos

The year is 175. In the morning your boat turns from the sea into the river and heads up the Eurymedon. Cargo ships wait on the bank, loading salt, olive oil and wool.

Reaching the city, you see arches striding across the plain. Water from the mountains rises under pressure between two tall towers and is piped into town.

In the afternoon you enter the new theatre. The marble seats still gleam, and above the doorway an inscription names the architect, Zenon. With seven thousand others you wait for the play to begin.

Today the same theatre stands almost as it did on its first day. On summer evenings it fills up again, this time for opera, ballet and concerts.

🚌 Getting there

Aspendos is in the Serik district of Antalya, near Belkıs, about 45 km from central Antalya. A signposted turn leads off the Antalya–Alanya highway; by public transport, go to Serik and take a taxi.

⭐ Don't miss

  • One of the best-preserved Roman theatres in the world, with its stage building
  • The basilica and agora remains on the acropolis
  • The Roman aqueduct across the plain
  • The Seljuk bridge over the Köprüçay nearby

👤 Notable people

  • The theatre was built in the 2nd century under Emperor Marcus Aurelius by the architect Zenon
  • The Seljuks also used it as a caravanserai in the 13th century

💡 Tips

The acoustics are famous: speak from the centre of the stage and listen from the upper rows. Check the programme of the summer opera and ballet festival in advance. Easy to combine with Perge and Side in one day.
