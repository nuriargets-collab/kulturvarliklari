Zonguldak Mining Museum tells the story of the city shaped by coal, where Türkiye's hard-coal mining began after coal was found in the mid-19th century. Miners' tools, helmets, lamps, clothing and models of techniques are displayed, while realistic galleries convey how hard and dangerous work hundreds of metres underground is – also a tribute to miners who lost their lives.
