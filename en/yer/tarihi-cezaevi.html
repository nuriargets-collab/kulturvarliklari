Sinop Historical Prison, inside Sinop fortress, was built in the mid-19th century and used until the 1990s. Housing long-sentence prisoners, it was said to be almost impossible to escape. Inmates included figures of Turkish literature such as Sabahattin Ali. Today wards, cells, courtyards and ramparts can be visited, with displays evoking the atmosphere – a thought-provoking experience.
