Perre Örenyeri

Home › Southeastern Anatolia › Adıyaman
Location Merkez, AdıyamanRegion Southeastern AnatoliaType Archaeological sitePeriod Kommagene / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Perre was one of the important towns of the Kingdom of Commagene and lies on a hillside very close to the centre of Adıyaman. Its main attraction is the large number of chamber tombs cut into the rock; lined up along the slope, they look like a neighbourhood of stone. A fountain structure and other remains from Roman times hint at daily life. Unlike busy major sites, Perre can be explored on a quiet walk, and its closeness to the city makes it ideal for a short visit.
