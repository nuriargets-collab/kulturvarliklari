Ziya Gökalp Müzesi

Home › Southeastern Anatolia › Diyarbakır
Location Sur, DiyarbakırRegion Southeastern AnatoliaType MuseumPeriod Osmanlı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Ziya Gökalp Museum occupies the house in Diyarbakır where the sociologist and writer Ziya Gökalp, an important figure in Turkish intellectual history, was born and grew up. The basalt, courtyard house reflects the setting of his childhood and youth. His books, manuscripts, photographs and belongings show the influence of his ideas in the late Ottoman and early Republican periods. Close to the Cahit Sıtkı Tarancı Museum.
