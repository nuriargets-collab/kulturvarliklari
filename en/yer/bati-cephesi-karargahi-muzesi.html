Batı Cephesi Karargahı Müzesi

The Western Front Headquarters Museum in Akşehir occupies the historic building used as headquarters of the Western Front before the Great Offensive, the final decisive phase of the War of Independence. In summer 1922 Mustafa Kemal Pasha and his commanders prepared the offensive here, and many decisions leading to victory were taken in this building. Commanders' offices, maps, documents, photographs and military items are displayed.
