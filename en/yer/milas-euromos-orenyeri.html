Milas Euromos Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › Muğla
Location Milas, MuğlaRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Euromos, on the road between Milas and Söke amid olive groves, is known for its Temple of Zeus, one of the best-preserved temples in Anatolia. Most of its Corinthian columns still stand, some bearing plaques naming their donors. A theatre, agora and walls show the city's former size; close to the main road, it makes an easy, photogenic stop.
