Yörük Ali Efe Evi Müzesi

Yörük Ali Efe House Museum honours Yörük Ali Efe, one of the leaders of local resistance against occupation in the Aegean in the first years of the War of Independence. Efes and zeybeks were communities of the Aegean highlands known for their distinctive dress and customs; Yörük Ali Efe became a symbol of the resistance that began in 1919. Photographs, documents, weapons and personal items tell his story.
