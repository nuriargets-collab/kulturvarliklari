Lagina Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › Muğla
Location Yatağan, MuğlaRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Lagina, near Yatağan, was one of antiquity's most important sanctuaries of the goddess Hecate, linked to nearby Stratonikeia by a sacred way along which annual processions passed. The Hellenistic Temple of Hecate, with friezes of gods and mythological scenes (partly in the Istanbul Archaeological Museums), is art-historically significant; the propylon, altar and stoas can also be seen.
