Sinop Arslantorunlar Etnografya Müzesi

Home › Black Sea › Sinop
Location Merkez, SinopRegion Black SeaType MuseumPeriod Osmanlı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Sinop Arslantorunlar Ethnography Museum is set in a two-storey, largely timber 19th-century Sinop mansion reflecting the domestic architecture of Black Sea towns. Period-furnished rooms display local costumes, embroidery, kitchenware, bridal trousseaus and items from Sinop's seafaring tradition.
