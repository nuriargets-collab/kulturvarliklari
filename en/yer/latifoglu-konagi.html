Latifoğlu Konağı

Home › Black Sea › Tokat
Location Merkez, TokatRegion Black SeaType MuseumPeriod Osmanlı (19. yy)
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Latifoğlu Mansion in Tokat, built in the 19th century, is regarded as one of the finest examples of Ottoman domestic architecture. Baroque-influenced decoration, painted ceilings, wood carving and a spacious hall reflect the life of wealthy families, while harem and selamlık sections show the Ottoman household layout; rooms are furnished with period scenes.
