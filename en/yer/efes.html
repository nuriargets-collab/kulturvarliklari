Ephesus was one of antiquity's greatest port cities and among the brightest centres of the Roman province of Asia; the Temple of Artemis, one of the Seven Wonders, stood nearby. Walking down marble-paved Curetes Street past fountains, temples, public latrines and shops, visitors follow daily life step by step. At its end the two-storey façade of the Library of Celsus is among the most photographed Roman buildings. The Great Theatre, seating about 25,000, the Harbour Street and the Terrace Houses complete the picture, while the House of the Virgin Mary and the Basilica of St John make it important for Christian history. UNESCO World Heritage since 2015.
