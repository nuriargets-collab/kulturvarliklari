Bursa Atatürk Evi Müzesi

Home › Marmara › Bursa
Location Osmangazi, BursaRegion MarmaraType MuseumPeriod Cumhuriyet
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Bursa Atatürk House Museum is an elegant late 19th-century mansion where Atatürk stayed on visits to Bursa. Set in a garden, it is a European-influenced example of the city's domestic architecture of the period. His belongings, furniture and photographs preserve the rooms as they were during his stays – a calm stop during a tour of historic Bursa.
