Bursa Türk İslam Eserleri Müzesi

Home › Marmara › Bursa
Location Yıldırım, BursaRegion MarmaraType MuseumPeriod Osmanlı
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Bursa Museum of Turkish and Islamic Arts is housed in the medrese of the Green Complex, a masterpiece of early Ottoman architecture built in the early 15th century by Sultan Mehmed I and named after the green-turquoise tiles of its mosque and tomb. Rooms around the arcaded courtyard display Seljuk and Ottoman tiles, ceramics, manuscripts, woodwork, metalwork, costumes and jewellery. Seeing the Green Mosque and Green Tomb opposite completes the experience.
