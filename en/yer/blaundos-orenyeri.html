Blaundos Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › Uşak
Location Ulubey, UşakRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Helenistik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Blaundos, in Ulubey district of Uşak, sits on a plateau almost surrounded by the deep valleys of the Ulubey Canyon, whose steep sides gave natural protection. A monumental gate, temple remains, a stadium and rock tombs cut into the canyon walls survive; the canyon's dramatic scenery makes the visit a meeting of history and nature.
