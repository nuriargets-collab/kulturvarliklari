Klaros Örenyeri

Home › Aegean › İzmir
Location Menderes, İzmirRegion AegeanType Archaeological sitePeriod Arkaik / Roma
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Klaros, in Menderes, is a sanctuary of Apollo that ranked with Delphi and Didyma among the most respected oracles of the ancient world. Delegations from distant cities came to put their questions to the god; the oracle was given in dark underground chambers beneath the temple by a priest who drank from the sacred spring. These vaulted passages can still be seen, and fragments of colossal statues of Apollo, Artemis and Leto are the site's most impressive finds. Among pines, often with rising groundwater, it has a mystical atmosphere.
