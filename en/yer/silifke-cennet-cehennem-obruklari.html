Silifke Cennet Cehennem Obrukları

Home › Mediterranean › Mersin
Location Silifke, MersinRegion MediterraneanType Natural sitePeriod Doğal Alan / Bizans
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

The Heaven and Hell sinkholes in Silifke are two giant collapse pits formed as underground water dissolved the limestone. Hundreds of steps lead down into lush Heaven, where a small Byzantine chapel dedicated to the Virgin Mary stands at the mouth of a cave echoing with an underground river. Nearby Hell, with almost vertical walls, cannot be entered; legend says Zeus imprisoned the monster Typhon here. Their contrast explains the names.
