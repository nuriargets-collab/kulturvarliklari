Gazi Müzesi

Home › Black Sea › Samsun
Location İlkadım, SamsunRegion Black SeaType MuseumPeriod Cumhuriyet (1919)
🧭 Get directions Apple Maps

Gazi Museum is housed in the former Mantika Palace Hotel, where Atatürk stayed after landing at Samsun on 19 May 1919 to begin the national struggle – a date celebrated each year as Atatürk Commemoration, Youth and Sports Day. His room, study and meeting hall are arranged with period items, photographs and documents.
