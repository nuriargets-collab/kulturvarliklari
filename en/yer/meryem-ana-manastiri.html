The Monastery of the Virgin Mary in Şebinkarahisar, Giresun, is an impressive rock monastery that seems to cling to a sheer cliff face. One of the fine examples of rock-cut religious buildings in the Black Sea region, it preserves traces of frescoes inside. Blending into the rock from afar, it surprises visitors with both its architecture and setting. The climb takes some effort, but the valley views and quiet atmosphere reward it.
