Hatay Archaeology Museum

Hatay Arkeoloji Müzesi

Hatay Archaeological Museum is famous for one of the world's richest mosaic collections. Antakya, ancient Antioch – one of the largest cities of the Roman Empire – and resorts such as Daphne (Harbiye) had villas adorned with mosaics of myths, hunts and daily life. Hittite and Neo-Hittite sculpture, Roman sarcophagi, coins and jewellery are also displayed. Antakya was severely affected by the 2023 earthquakes; check the museum's current status before visiting.
