Gaziantep Castle rises on an artificial mound in the city centre, its origins thought to reach back to Roman watch posts. Enlarged in Byzantine times and repaired by the Mamluks and Ottomans, it now houses a panorama museum about the long, heroic defence of Antep against occupation in 1920–21, after which the city received the title 'Gazi'. The coppersmiths' bazaar, inns and historic streets around it are an ideal start for exploring Gaziantep's crafts and cuisine.
