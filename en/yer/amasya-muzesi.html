Amasya Müzesi

Amasya Museum tells the long history of this fairy-tale city on both banks of the Yeşilırmak, famous for its royal rock tombs. Amasya was the capital of the Kingdom of Pontus and birthplace of the geographer Strabo; later it flourished under the Danishmendids and Seljuks and, in Ottoman times, as a province where princes were trained. Alongside finds from the Bronze Age to the Ottomans, its medieval mummies – rare in Anatolia – draw the most attention.
