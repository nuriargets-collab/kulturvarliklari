Panorama 1453 Tarih Museum

Panorama 1453 Tarih Müzesi

The Panorama 1453 History Museum, right beside the walls at Topkapı, recreates the final moment of the 1453 siege of Constantinople in a vast 360-degree painting. Standing on the platform under the dome, with cannon fire and battle sounds, visitors feel inside the siege. Information panels on the lower floor tell the story. The museum opened in 2009. The panoramic painting is blended with real objects in the foreground so that visitors feel they are in the midst of the siege.
