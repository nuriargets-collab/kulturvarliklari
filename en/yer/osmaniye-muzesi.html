Osmaniye Museum presents Osmaniye at the eastern end of Çukurova, between the Taurus and Amanus mountains, on routes linking Anatolia and Syria. Finds from Karatepe, Kastabala and other sites – coins, sculpture – and local ethnography introduce a region settled in Late Hittite, Cilician, Roman and medieval times. As the region was affected by the 2023 earthquakes, check current opening status before visiting.
