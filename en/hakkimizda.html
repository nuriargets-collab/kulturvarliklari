About

Kültür Varlıkları is an independent cultural guide presenting Türkiye's museums and archaeological sites in one place – searchable, mappable and with audio narration.

Every place has an originally written history, narration in Turkish, English and German, a map, directions and nearby places; ready-made routes and an AI guide help you plan your trip.

For opening hours, tickets and temporary closures, always check official sources. This is not the official website of the Ministry of Culture and Tourism.
