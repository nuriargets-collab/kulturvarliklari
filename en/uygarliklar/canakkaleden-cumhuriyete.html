From Gallipoli to the Republic

Civilizations and periods › From Gallipoli to the Republic
Period 1915 – 1923Places 14Reading 1 min🔊 Audio🎓 Take the quiz

From Gallipoli to the Republic. The years from the Gallipoli campaign to the War of Independence and the founding of the Republic live on in museums and houses across Anatolia. The naval and land battles of 1915 in the Dardanelles during the First World War cost both sides dearly; the naval victory of 18 March 1915 is commemorated every year. Kilitbahir Fortress, the Hamidiye and Namazgâh batteries and the Çanakkale Epic Promotion Centre tell the story of this period. The National Struggle, which began when Mustafa Kemal Pasha landed at Samsun on 19 May 1919, was organised through the Erzurum and Sivas congresses, and on 23 April 1920 the Grand National Assembly opened in Ankara. The Gazi Museum in Samsun, the Sivas Congress Museum and the War of Independence Museum (the first Assembly building) in Ankara are stops on this journey, while the Kuva-yi Milliye Museum in Balıkesir and the Western Front Headquarters Museum in Konya show other sides of the war. The house where the Armistice of Mudanya was signed after the Great Offensive of 1922 is now a museum. The Republic was proclaimed on 29 October 1923; the Republic Museum in Ankara, in the second Assembly building, tells of the young Republic's early years. Many provinces also have house museums where Atatürk stayed.
